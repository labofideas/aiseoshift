# Professional AI Tools Content Plan

20 professions × 5 use cases = 120 potential posts (20 pillars + 100 spokes).

## Strategy

- **Phase 1 — Pilots:** Ship 20 pillar pages (one per profession). Same listicle format as the existing AI tooling posts (20 tools, TL;DR, TOC, comparison table, 15 FAQs, schema, hero image, OG tags).
- **Phase 2 — Double down:** After 4-8 weeks of GSC data, identify top-3 ranking professions. Ship the 5 use-case spokes for each, building internal-link topic clusters.
- **Monetization:** SEO services CTA at end of each post.
- **Slug rule:** `ai-tools-for-<profession>` (no stop words, no year).

## Post template (per pillar)

Same format as `/blog/ai-paraphrasing-tools/` baseline. Key elements:

1. **Frontmatter:** title, description, deck, pubDate, category=Tooling, tags (include profession name), author, featured (optional)
2. **Intro** (200-300 words): Frame the AI shift for this profession. Tie to "search is shifting to AI" thesis.
3. **TL;DR / Quick answer** block
4. **TOC** with anchor links to every tool + section
5. **Definitional sections:** What AI tools do for [profession], How they work in 2026, Ranking criteria
6. **20 tools** — each with: Website link, 1-paragraph description, Key features (5 bullets), Pros (3) / Cons (2), Pricing, Best for
7. **Comparison table** (20 rows)
8. **How to choose for your team/practice** (use the profession's vocabulary)
9. **Best practices** (profession-specific)
10. **15 FAQs** (FAQPage-schema-friendly)
11. **"How [profession]s are getting found in the AI-search era"** — short connector section linking to E-E-A-T, AI citations, AEO posts. **SEO services CTA here.**
12. **Related reading** (5-6 internal links)
13. **References**
14. **Disclosure** (if affiliate links present)

## Notes & risks

- **Overlap with existing posts:** Some Phase 2 spokes overlap with the 12 published listicles (e.g. `ai-tools-for-image-generation` vs `ai-image-generators`). For these, either skip the spoke or differentiate by audience framing.
- **Affiliate revenue:** Pure SEO play in Phase 1. Monetize via SEO-services CTA. Phase 2 may reveal affiliate opportunities once we see what tools dominate per niche.
- **Accuracy:** Per-tool pricing and feature claims kept general; spot-check before each batch publishes.
- **Image strategy:** Reuse the `scripts/fetch-new-post-images.mjs` pattern. Profession-specific Pexels queries.
