CBD Industry SEO Landscape

Understanding the unique challenges and opportunities in CBD/hemp marketing

💡 Why CBD SEO is Different

CBD brands face unique digital marketing challenges that make SEO even more critical than for typical ecommerce. With paid advertising restrictions on Google, Facebook, and Instagram, organic search becomes the primary sustainable traffic channel.

Industry Challenges Hempeutics Must Navigate

🚫 Ad Restrictions

Google Ads, Facebook, Instagram prohibit most CBD advertising. SEO is not optional; it's the primary traffic channel.

⚖️ Compliance

FDA regulations restrict health claims. Content must be educational without making therapeutic promises.

🏦 Payment Hurdles

High-risk merchant status affects trust signals. Third-party verification becomes crucial for credibility.

Why Hempeutics Has a Competitive Moat

Most CBD brands are faceless ecommerce operations. Hempeutics has two assets that 95% of competitors lack:

👩‍⚕️ Licensed Pharmacist Founder Dr. Pegah Panahi's credentials provide legitimate medical authority. This is E-E-A-T gold in an industry full of questionable claims.
🏪 Physical Clinic Location A real address at Pacifica Medical Tower creates local SEO opportunities and reinforces legitimacy.
