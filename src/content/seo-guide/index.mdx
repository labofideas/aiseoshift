SEO in 2026 is not what it was five years ago. The fundamentals still matter, but the landscape has expanded. Google's AI Overviews now appear on half of all US searches. ChatGPT processes 900 million weekly queries. Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini have become legitimate discovery channels. And 65 percent of Google searches end without a click.

The websites that thrive in this environment do not just rank in Google. They get cited by AI answer engines, maintain visibility across multiple platforms, and build the kind of authority that both algorithms and language models recognize.

This guide covers every dimension of modern SEO across 12 chapters. It is designed for practitioners who need practical, current guidance, not theory or nostalgia for how search used to work. Each chapter stands alone as a reference, but they build on each other if you read them in order.

## Who this guide is for

If you manage a website and want it found, this guide is for you. That includes SEO specialists adapting to AI search, content marketers watching traffic patterns shift, developers building sites that need to be crawlable by both Googlebot and GPTBot, business owners trying to understand why their search traffic is declining despite stable rankings, and anyone starting from zero who wants to learn SEO as it actually works today.

## How to use this guide

Each chapter covers one major SEO discipline at reference depth and links to our deeper blog posts where we cover specific subtopics in full detail. Start with Chapter 1 if you are new. Jump to the chapter that matches your current challenge if you are experienced.

The first seven chapters cover the foundations that every site needs. Chapters 8 through 12 cover the AI-era disciplines that separate sites that grow from sites that stagnate.
