A patient looking for a new dentist in 2026 does not start with the Yellow Pages or even a Google search for "dentist near me." They ask ChatGPT for a recommendation. They ask Perplexity which practice in their zip accepts their insurance. Google AI Overviews summarize "best dentist in [city]" before any links appear. The dental practices cited in those AI answers fill chairs. The ones that are not cited compete for the scraps.

This is the playbook for becoming a cited practice. Four pillars, each implementable whether you run a solo practice or a multi-location DSO.

## Quick answer

The four things that drive dental SEO in 2026: a deeply optimized Google Business Profile per location, a systematic review velocity program, procedure-specific landing pages authored by licensed dentists, and dental [schema markup](/seo/schema-markup/) that makes the practice and providers machine-readable. Most dental practices are doing none of these well. The ones that compound all four dominate their markets.

For a structured engagement, our [AI SEO services for dentists and dental practices](/ai-seo-services/dentists/) page covers audits, patient discovery optimization, and ongoing retainers.

## Why dental SEO changed in 2026

Three shifts:

1. **AI Overviews answer dental questions directly.** "Does teeth whitening damage enamel," "how much do dental implants cost," "are veneers worth it." The practices cited as sources get brand impression. Everyone else gets nothing.
2. **ChatGPT and Perplexity recommend practices.** Conversational queries like "who is the best dentist in [zip]" now return actual practice recommendations. They are pulled from review profiles, structured data, and content.
3. **YMYL scrutiny on health content.** Dental health falls under Google's Your Money or Your Life category. Practices without proper [E-E-A-T signals](/seo/eeat/) and dentist-attributed content are filtered out of AI recommendations.

For broader context, see our [AI SEO Shift pillar](/ai-seo-shift/).

## Pillar 1: Google Business Profile

GBP is the highest-converting surface for dental practices. New patients check it before they call.

The non-negotiables per location:

- Primary category: Dentist (or Dental Clinic for multi-provider practices)
- Secondary categories: every applicable one (Cosmetic Dentist, Pediatric Dentist, Orthodontist, Endodontist, Periodontist, Emergency Dental Service)
- Insurance accepted (specific carriers, not just "most insurance")
- Same-day appointment availability if offered
- 30+ photos including team, office, equipment, and waiting area
- Weekly posts (new patient promotions, dental health tips, technology highlights)
- Q&A seeded with common patient questions
- Active review velocity system

The companion post on [Google Business Profile optimization for dental practices](/blog/google-business-profile-for-dentists/) covers the tactical details.

## Pillar 2: Review velocity

Reviews drive both the local pack and AI engine recommendations for healthcare providers. The mechanics:

### Post-appointment SMS request

Within 4 to 24 hours of the appointment, while the experience is fresh. Personalized with patient name and the dentist who saw them.

### Direct Google review link

Skip the friction of asking patients to search for your practice. Send the direct review link.

### 24-hour response window

Every review, positive and negative. HIPAA-safe responses that never confirm patient status or share treatment details.

### Cross-platform consistency

Patients check Google, Yelp, Healthgrades, and ZocDoc. Active presence on all four signals trustworthiness.

The companion post on [patient reviews and dental SEO](/blog/patient-reviews-dental-seo-velocity/) covers the full HIPAA-compliant operating system.

## Pillar 3: Procedure-specific landing pages

Patients search by procedure, not by practice. Each major procedure deserves its own page authored by a licensed dentist.

Procedures that should each have their own page:

- Dental cleanings and exams
- Dental implants
- Root canals
- Crowns and bridges
- Veneers
- Teeth whitening (in-office and take-home)
- Invisalign and clear aligners
- Wisdom teeth removal
- Emergency dental care
- Periodontal treatment
- Pediatric dentistry
- Sedation dentistry

Each page should cover what the procedure is, who needs it, what to expect, recovery, cost ranges (with insurance considerations), and FAQs. Bylined to the dentist who performs it.

The companion post on [dental schema and procedure pages](/blog/dental-schema-procedure-pages/) covers the structure and schema implementation.

## Pillar 4: Dental schema and credentials

YMYL scrutiny means structured data matters. Every dental practice needs:

- `Dentist` schema on the practice homepage
- `Person` schema with `hasCredential` for each dentist
- `MedicalProcedure` schema for each procedure page
- `FAQPage` schema on procedure and FAQ pages
- `Review` schema on the homepage and dentist bio pages

For implementation, see the [dental schema post](/blog/dental-schema-procedure-pages/) and the broader [schema markup guide](/seo/schema-markup/).

## The query types that book appointments

Stop chasing "dentist in [city]." It is a $15 to $40 CPC head term dominated by directory sites and DSO networks. The queries that book new patients are deeper:

- **Procedure + location:** "dental implants [city]," "Invisalign [city]"
- **Insurance + location:** "Aetna dentist [city]," "MetLife dentist [city]"
- **Specialty + location:** "pediatric dentist [city]," "cosmetic dentist [city]"
- **Question + procedure:** "how long does Invisalign take," "are dental implants worth it"
- **Emergency + location:** "emergency dentist near me," "broken tooth [city]"

These have lower volume but higher intent and lower competition. They are also the queries AI engines synthesize answers for.

## What this looks like operationally

A 90-day plan:

- **Month 1:** Full GBP audit and rebuild. Review velocity system live. First 5 procedure pages with dentist bylines.
- **Month 2:** 5 more procedure pages. Schema deployment sitewide. Insurance and specialty pages.
- **Month 3:** Citation cleanup. Pediatric or cosmetic specialty content. Performance review.

Twelve months in: 15+ procedure pages, 100+ recent reviews, full dental schema and dentist credentials infrastructure, and new patient calls from queries DSO competitors are not even targeting.

## FAQs

### How long does dental SEO take to show results?

Most practices see measurable improvement in three to six months. Local pack and GBP work shows fastest results — often within 60 to 90 days. Procedure-specific pages can rank within four to eight weeks for less-competitive long-tail queries.

### Is dental content always YMYL?

Yes. Anything related to health, treatment, or medical decisions falls under YMYL. Treat every clinical page as if it is being evaluated for licensed dentist authorship and accuracy.

### Should I name individual dentists or just use the practice name?

Named dentists, always. Anonymous "our team" bylines fail YMYL extraction. Each dentist on staff should byline content in their specialty area.

### What about DSO-controlled marketing?

DSO-controlled marketing is constrained by what the DSO permits. Most allow location-specific content and reviews. Negotiate for schema customization and bylined content even when broader site structure is centralized.

### Can I use AI to write procedure pages?

For first drafts, yes. For clinical accuracy, the named licensed dentist must contribute substantively. AI-drafted health content without licensed review fails YMYL extraction.
