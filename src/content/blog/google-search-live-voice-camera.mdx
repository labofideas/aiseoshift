Google launched Search Live in the US and expanded it to over 200 countries by March 2026. The feature lets users have real-time voice conversations within AI Mode and share their phone camera feed for contextual, visual AI answers.

This is not voice search as it existed before. The old voice search converted speech to text and ran a standard query. Search Live is a continuous conversation with a visual AI that can see what you see.

## How Search Live works

Open Google Search on your phone. Below the search box, you now see shortcuts to AI Mode, Talk (Search Live voice), and Create (image generation via Lens).

Tap Talk and start speaking. Google's AI responds conversationally, maintaining context across follow-up questions. You can interrupt, redirect, and refine your query in real time, just like talking to a person.

Tap the camera icon during a conversation and point your phone at something. Google's AI interprets the live video feed and responds based on what it sees. Point it at a plant and ask "what is this and how do I care for it?" Point it at a restaurant menu in another language and ask "translate this and tell me what is good here." Point it at a broken appliance and ask "how do I fix this?"

The system runs on Gemini 3.5 Flash and processes visual input at the speed of conversation. The responses are contextual, not just visual recognition. Google combines what it sees through your camera with what it knows from the web to generate useful, specific answers.

## What this means for content and SEO

Search Live changes the query pattern fundamentally. Users are not typing keywords. They are not even speaking keywords. They are having conversations about things they are looking at in the real world.

**Visual content becomes searchable context.** When someone points their camera at your product in a store, Google's AI will pull information about that product from the web. If your product pages have comprehensive, accurate, structured information, you get referenced. If they do not, a competitor's information gets used instead.

**Local search becomes ambient.** A user walking down a street can ask "what is that restaurant and is it any good?" while pointing their camera at it. Google pulls from the business's Google Business Profile, reviews, and website content to answer in real time. [Local SEO](/blog/local-seo-checklist-2026/) just became even more critical.

**How-to content gets consumed differently.** Someone pointing their camera at a home repair problem and asking "how do I fix this?" will get an AI-generated answer that may reference your tutorial content. But they will never visit your website. The content is consumed through the AI, not through your page. [Structured content that AI can extract](/seo/on-page-seo/) becomes the only content that reaches these users.

**Product information accuracy matters more.** If your product data is outdated, incomplete, or inconsistent across platforms, Search Live will either skip you or present inaccurate information. [Schema markup](/seo/schema-markup/) with current pricing, availability, and specifications is no longer optional for any business with physical products.

## The voice and visual search opportunity

Search Live represents the first mainstream integration of voice, vision, and AI in a single search experience. For businesses that adapt their content strategy to serve this format, the opportunity is significant.

The users engaging with Search Live are high-intent. They are looking at something real and asking a specific question about it. They are further along the decision-making process than someone typing a generic keyword. If your content answers their question, you reach a customer at the moment of maximum intent.

The businesses that win in Search Live are the ones with comprehensive, structured, accurate information across their website, Google Business Profile, and product databases. The technology is new. The fundamentals that power it are the same ones that power every other aspect of [AI search visibility](/ai-seo-shift/).
