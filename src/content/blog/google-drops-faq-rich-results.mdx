Google deprecated FAQ rich results on May 7, 2026. The visual dropdown that displayed question-and-answer pairs directly in search results is gone for most sites. Only medical and government pages retain them.

Search Console reporting for FAQ rich results ends in June. Rich Results Test support ends alongside it. Search Console API support ends in August. Google is removing the feature completely.

The SEO community reacted predictably. "FAQ schema is dead." "Remove your FAQ markup." "Stop wasting time on FAQ content."

Every one of those reactions is wrong. Here is why.

## What Google actually removed

Google removed a display feature, not a schema type. FAQ rich results were the visual treatment: the expandable question-and-answer accordion that appeared below your listing in search results. That visual treatment is gone.

FAQPage structured data, the JSON-LD markup that powered those rich results, remains a valid and recognized schema type on [Schema.org](https://schema.org/). Google explicitly stated it will continue using FAQ structured data to understand pages, even though the rich result feature is gone. The markup stays on your pages without causing problems.

This distinction matters enormously. The schema is the signal. The rich result was just one way that signal was displayed. The signal still works. The display changed.

## Why FAQ schema is now more valuable, not less

Here is the data point that most coverage missed.

Pages with FAQPage schema are 3.2 times more likely to appear in Google AI Overviews than pages without it. That number comes from analysis of AI Overview citations across thousands of queries. FAQ structured data has shifted from a Google rich result trigger to an AI citation signal.

Think about what that means. The old value of FAQ schema was a visual enhancement in search results that improved click-through rate by maybe 10 to 20 percent. The new value is a 3.2x increase in probability of appearing in AI Overviews, which reach 2.5 billion monthly users.

The old value was cosmetic. The new value is structural.

ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, and Gemini all parse FAQ structured data as a primary extraction source for Q&A answers. When an AI system encounters a page with properly marked-up FAQ content, it can extract each question-answer pair as a standalone fact with high confidence. That extraction confidence translates directly into citation probability.

## What to do now

**Do not remove FAQ schema from your pages.** The markup is still valid, still crawlable, and now more valuable for AI visibility than it was for rich results.

**Keep adding FAQ sections to important pages.** The content itself, clear questions with direct answers, is exactly what AI systems need to generate accurate responses and cite your content.

**Keep using FAQPage JSON-LD.** Implement it on every page that has question-and-answer content. The [schema implementation](/seo/schema-markup/) is unchanged. Only the visual display in Google search results changed.

**Update your measurement expectations.** You will no longer see FAQ rich result impressions in Search Console. But you should see the AI visibility impact through citation tracking. If you are using [AEO tools](/blog/best-aeo-geo-tools-2026/) like Semrush AI Toolkit or Otterly, track whether your FAQ-enabled pages get cited more frequently than pages without FAQ markup.

**Consider adding FAQ sections to pages that do not have them.** If FAQ schema now primarily serves AI visibility rather than rich results, the pages that benefit most are your highest-value commercial pages, not just informational content. Product pages, service pages, and landing pages with FAQ sections and schema are now positioned for AI Overview inclusion.

## The bigger picture

Google is systematically decoupling structured data from visual search features while simultaneously increasing its importance for AI systems. [Schema markup](/seo/schema-markup/) is becoming less about how your listing looks in Google and more about how AI systems understand and cite your content.

This is consistent with the broader [AI SEO shift](/ai-seo-shift/). The visual SERP is becoming less important as AI Overviews and AI answer engines handle a growing share of discovery. The structured data layer is becoming more important as the primary language through which your content communicates with machines.

The sites that remove FAQ schema because the rich result disappeared are optimizing for yesterday. The sites that keep it and expand it are optimizing for where search is going.
