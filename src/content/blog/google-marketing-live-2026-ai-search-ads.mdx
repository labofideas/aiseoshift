Google held [Google Marketing Live 2026](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/google-marketing-live-2026-collection/) on May 20, 2026 and laid out a search advertising roadmap that completes the shift from keyword-targeted text ads to Gemini-generated, conversational creative running inside AI Mode. Three new ad formats, a unified AI agent for advertisers, the retirement of Dynamic Search Ads, and a new commerce protocol were all announced in a single hour. If you sell anything through Google Search, the work you do for the rest of 2026 is now defined by what was on that stage.

This post explains every announcement that matters, the data Google cited, what the changes mean for SEO and AEO practitioners, and the concrete actions to take this quarter. External links throughout point to primary sources and to deeper expert analysis so you can dig further on any specific piece.

## TL;DR

Google is replacing keyword-matched text ads with Gemini-generated ads that respond to a user's specific question. The new formats live inside AI Mode in Search, the Gemini-powered conversational search experience that has reached the point where, per [Ipsos research Google cited on stage](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/google-marketing-live-search-ads/), 75% of users say they make faster, more confident decisions using it. Dynamic Search Ads will be auto-upgraded to AI Max in September 2026. Performance Max and AI Max are now the foundation every Google Search advertiser is expected to be on. Ask Advisor, a single Gemini agent, now spans Google Ads, Google Analytics, Merchant Center, and Google Marketing Platform.

## What Google announced at Marketing Live 2026

### Three new Gemini-generated ad formats

**Conversational Discovery ads.** When a user asks AI Mode a specific question like "what home fragrance works best in small apartments," Gemini will assemble ad creative tailored to that question on the fly, pulling from the advertiser's product feed, landing pages, and brand assets. The advertiser does not write the ad. The system writes the ad in response to the query. Coverage at [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-marketing-live-2026-everything-you-need-to-know-478167) goes into greater detail on how creative is assembled.

**Highlighted Answers.** Inside AI Mode's generated recommendation lists, high-quality ads can now appear as one of the recommended items. Google's example was a query about learning a language for an upcoming trip: a language-app ad surfaces directly inside the AI's recommendation, not separately beneath it. This is the most aggressive blurring of paid and organic in Search's history.

**AI-powered Shopping ads.** Gemini generates a custom explainer next to each shopping ad describing why that specific product matches the searcher's intent. Static product titles and descriptions become dynamic, query-aware sales copy. Launching in the coming months.

### AI Max becomes the foundation

[As AI Max turns one](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/google-marketing-live-2026-collection/), Google announced that to appear in any of the new AI Search ad experiences advertisers must be running AI Max for Search Campaigns or Performance Max with text customization. The data Google cited on stage: Lufthansa Group saw a 24% increase in return on ad spend with AI Max, and IKEA reported a 65% lift in non-branded clicks alongside a 28% boost in incremental ROAS. [PPC Land's coverage](https://ppc.land/google-marketing-live-2026-every-announcement-that-actually-matters/) lays out the case for advertisers that have not yet migrated.

Two more AI Max details that matter:

- **Dynamic Search Ads will be auto-upgraded to AI Max in September 2026.** A format that defined search automation for over a decade is being retired. If you still rely on DSA for long-tail coverage, the migration is now mandatory.
- **AI Max for Shopping** brings the same one-click toggle to Shopping campaigns, replacing manual feed-driven targeting with Gemini's query understanding.

### Ask Advisor: one agent across the entire stack

[Ask Advisor](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/google-marketing-live-search-ads/) is a single Gemini-powered agent that spans Google Ads, Google Analytics, Merchant Center, and Google Marketing Platform. Instead of opening four interfaces and stitching together a campaign analysis, an advertiser can ask Ask Advisor for the answer in plain English and act on it without leaving the conversation. [Monks' analysis](https://www.monks.com/articles/steering-machine-our-take-agentic-shift-google-marketing-live-2026) calls this the agency-side version of the same agentic shift consumers are experiencing in AI Mode.

### Direct Offers, native checkout, and Universal Commerce Protocol

Direct Offers, which launched in January 2026 with Chewy, Gap, and L'Oreal, is expanding with promotion bundling (multiple discount types stacked together by AI), native in-ad checkout for merchants on the Universal Commerce Protocol, and a travel expansion through partners like Booking and Expedia. The thesis: the ad and the transaction will increasingly happen in the same surface.

### A Business Agent for lead-gen

Google is also testing a chatbot-style "Business Agent for Leads" that replaces static lead forms with a conversational engagement layer inside the ad itself. The user has a multi-turn conversation with the business before ever clicking through to a website.

## Why this matters for SEO and AEO

Google Marketing Live is a paid product event, but the implications for organic search and answer engine optimization are immediate.

### Paid and organic are colliding inside AI Mode

Until now, the dividing line between SEO and SEM has been visually obvious: organic results in one section, ads in another. AI Mode is collapsing that distinction. A Highlighted Answer ad does not look different from an organically surfaced recommendation. Conversational Discovery ads are written in the same voice and format as AI-generated answers. For users this means the paid-vs-organic distinction is being abstracted away; for marketers it means the discipline of getting cited in AI answers ([AEO and GEO](/seo/ai-search-optimization/)) now competes for the same screen real estate as paid placement.

### Strong product feeds and brand assets are now paid-search infrastructure

Conversational Discovery ads and AI-powered Shopping ads are generated by Gemini, but they generate from somewhere. That somewhere is your product feed, your landing page copy, your structured data, and your brand assets. The same structured, AI-extractable content that earns organic citations is what fuels the new ad formats. A site with thin product descriptions, missing schema, and weak brand assets will lose in both lanes. See our chapter on [schema markup and structured data](/seo/schema-markup/) for the underlying foundation.

### Measurement is moving from positions to citations and incremental lift

IKEA's reported 28% boost in incremental ROAS is the metric Google is now leading with, not "rank position" or even "click-through rate." Incremental lift, brand share of voice inside AI Mode answers, and citation frequency are becoming the canonical KPIs. This is the same direction organic [SEO measurement](/seo/measuring-seo/) is already moving for AI search.

### Long-tail keyword targeting is being phased out

Dynamic Search Ads were the closest paid analogue to organic long-tail capture. Replacing them with AI Max means Google's models, not your keyword lists, decide which queries to bid on. The implication for SEO: long-tail keyword research is shifting from "list the queries" to "structure the content so any phrasing of the user's intent can match." That is much closer to how AI engines retrieve content already.

## What advertisers should do this quarter

The actions are concrete and time-bound.

1. **Migrate Dynamic Search Ads to AI Max now.** The forced migration happens in September 2026. Migrating early lets you tune budgets and creative assets before competitors do.
2. **Audit your product feed and landing pages.** Gemini-generated ads pull from these. Add unique product copy, complete attribute coverage, and schema. Thin feeds will produce thin ads.
3. **Adopt AI Max for Search and AI Max for Shopping in every account.** Performance Max with text customization is the minimum to appear in the new AI surfaces.
4. **Test Ask Advisor with your team.** Treat it as a planning surface, not a reporting surface. The advantage compounds for accounts that learn to brief it well.
5. **Prepare for Direct Offers and native checkout.** If you operate ecommerce, evaluate joining the Universal Commerce Protocol so transactions can complete inside the ad surface.
6. **Reframe reporting to incremental lift and brand presence in AI Mode.** Position-based reporting is becoming a lagging indicator.

## What SEO practitioners should watch

For organic-focused teams, four signals to monitor:

- **AI Mode citation rate** for your brand and top product queries. This is the new "share of voice."
- **Highlighted Answer placements** appearing for queries you used to win organically. When they do, your content is now competing with a Gemini-generated ad inside what used to be your slot.
- **Product feed and structured data quality**, because the same investments now serve both organic AEO and paid Conversational Discovery ads. The work overlaps more than it conflicts.
- **Brand entity strength.** Gemini-generated ads pull from brand assets and trusted reference data. Sites with weak [E-E-A-T signals](/seo/eeat/) and unclear entity definitions will get weaker AI-generated creative.

## The bigger pattern

Three years ago paid search was keyword-targeted text ads against a list of phrases. Today the leading format is a model-generated answer assembled in real time from your structured content. The skills that win in this world are the ones AEO practitioners already developed for AI Overviews and ChatGPT Search: structured pages, strong product data, clear entity signals, and content that can be retrieved and reassembled by an AI without breaking. SEO and SEM are converging into a single discipline of being the most extractable source in your category. Google Marketing Live 2026 is not a list of new ad products; it is Google formally telling marketers that the future ad surface is a Gemini answer.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is AI Max for Search Campaigns?**
AI Max is a setting in Google Ads that lets Gemini decide which queries to bid on, generate the ad creative dynamically, and optimize toward business outcomes rather than keyword-level performance. Google announced at Marketing Live 2026 that it is now the foundation required to appear in the new AI Mode ad surfaces.

**When are Dynamic Search Ads going away?**
Dynamic Search Ads will be auto-upgraded to AI Max in September 2026. Advertisers can migrate manually before then to control the transition; after that date the migration is forced.

**What are the three new Gemini-generated ad formats?**
Conversational Discovery ads (creative assembled in response to a specific question), Highlighted Answers (ads surfaced inside AI Mode recommendation lists), and AI-powered Shopping ads (Gemini-generated product explainers next to each shopping result).

**Does Google Marketing Live 2026 affect organic SEO?**
Yes. AI Mode now mixes paid Highlighted Answers into the same recommendation lists where organic citations appear, so paid and organic compete for the same surface. The structured content, schema, and product feeds that earn organic AI citations also fuel the new Gemini-generated ad formats, so the underlying investments overlap.

**What is Ask Advisor in Google Ads?**
Ask Advisor is a Gemini-powered agent that spans Google Ads, Google Analytics, Merchant Center, and Google Marketing Platform. It is a conversational interface that lets an advertiser ask cross-product questions and take action without switching tools.
