OpenAI overhauled the ChatGPT subscription lineup in early 2026. Free users got larger context windows and limited access to GPT-5. Plus subscribers now see higher message caps and Sora 2 video generation. A new Business tier sits between Plus and Enterprise with team features but friendlier pricing than the legacy Team plan. Enterprise pricing expanded to include dedicated GPUs and reserved capacity for large customers.

If you have been paying $20 per month since 2023 and wondering whether the plan still makes sense, or you are trying to choose between Plus, Pro, and Business for your team, this guide breaks down every current ChatGPT plan in 2026 — what you get, what you pay, and which one fits each use case.

## ChatGPT plans at a glance

| Plan | Monthly Price | Best Model | Message Cap | Team Features | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | GPT-5 Mini | Limited | No | Casual daily use |
| Plus | $20 | GPT-5 | Expanded | No | Power users and creators |
| Pro | $200 | GPT-5 Pro, unlimited | Unlimited | No | Researchers and heavy users |
| Business | $25 per user | GPT-5 | Expanded | Yes | Small and mid-size teams |
| Enterprise | Custom | GPT-5 Pro | Unlimited | Yes, SSO, audit | Large organizations |

Below we break down what each tier actually includes, what changed in 2026, and how to choose.

## ChatGPT Free in 2026

The free tier is no longer a stripped-down demo. In 2026, OpenAI expanded free access to make ChatGPT competitive with Google's Gemini Free and Anthropic's Claude free tier.

**What you get on Free:**

- Access to GPT-5 Mini, a faster and lighter version of the flagship model
- Limited access to full GPT-5 during off-peak hours
- Standard voice mode (text-to-speech and speech-to-text)
- Web browsing and basic image analysis
- Custom GPTs (use only, not creation)
- 10 DALL-E 3 image generations per day
- Memory for conversation context

**What you do not get:**

- Priority access during high-traffic hours
- Advanced voice mode
- Sora 2 video generation
- Priority access to new features
- Higher message limits on GPT-5
- Team or admin controls

The free tier is now genuinely useful for occasional users, students, and anyone who wants to try AI without a subscription. For daily professional work, the message limits will still push most users toward Plus.

## ChatGPT Plus — $20 per month

Plus remains OpenAI's most popular tier. The 2026 update kept the $20 price point but significantly expanded what you get for it.

**What is new on Plus in 2026:**

- **Sora 2 video generation** included (limited monthly quota)
- **Higher GPT-5 message limits** — roughly 5x what Free users get
- **Priority access to new models** during launch windows
- **Extended context window** up to 128K tokens on most conversations
- **Advanced voice mode** with natural interruption and tone matching
- **Custom GPTs creation** (not just usage)
- **Faster response times** during peak hours

**Who Plus is best for:**

- Freelancers and creators using ChatGPT daily for writing, coding, or research
- Students working on long-form projects with large context needs
- Marketers generating content and visuals regularly
- Developers using ChatGPT for code review and debugging

If you hit GPT-5 limits on Free more than once a week, Plus pays for itself. For most individual knowledge workers, $20 per month is still the best value in the lineup.

## ChatGPT Pro — $200 per month

Pro launched in late 2024 at $200 per month, and the 2026 update sharpened its positioning. It is now the tier for researchers, scientists, and professionals who need the absolute best model quality and unlimited usage.

**What is included on Pro:**

- **Unlimited GPT-5 access** with no daily message caps
- **GPT-5 Pro** — the reasoning-heavy variant that spends more compute per response on complex problems
- **Unlimited Sora 2 video generation** within reasonable use
- **Advanced voice mode** with priority bandwidth
- **128K context window** as default
- **Deep research mode** — ChatGPT runs multi-step research queries across the web, reading and synthesizing dozens of sources
- **Priority access** to experimental features like operator mode and computer use
- **Early access** to new models weeks before Plus tier

**Who Pro is worth it for:**

- Academic researchers running deep literature reviews
- Lawyers using ChatGPT for document analysis at scale
- Product managers running extensive user research synthesis
- Developers building on top of ChatGPT with heavy daily usage
- Creators generating Sora 2 video at scale

For most users, Pro is overkill. But if you are paying for two or three other AI tools plus Plus, consolidating to Pro often saves money and delivers better output on the hardest problems.

## ChatGPT Business — $25 per user per month

Business is the new 2026 tier designed to replace the older Team plan. It sits between Plus and Enterprise with team features, admin controls, and a friendlier price than Enterprise.

**What is included on Business:**

- Everything in Plus, per user
- **Shared workspace** where team members can collaborate on GPTs, prompts, and projects
- **Admin console** for user management and billing
- **Data privacy guarantee** — your data is not used to train OpenAI models
- **Higher message limits** than Plus
- **SSO support** for Google Workspace and Microsoft 365
- **Shared custom GPTs** within the workspace
- **Usage analytics** for team administrators

**Who Business is best for:**

- Marketing teams of 5 to 50 people
- Agencies wanting shared prompts and GPTs across contributors
- Engineering teams using ChatGPT for code review and documentation
- Any small or mid-size team that wants team features without Enterprise pricing

The data privacy guarantee alone is why most teams upgrade from Plus to Business. Plus subscriptions have opt-out training settings, but Business is no-training by default — an important distinction for regulated industries, legal teams, and client work.

## ChatGPT Enterprise — Custom pricing

Enterprise is for large organizations that need dedicated capacity, advanced security, and custom integrations. OpenAI does not publish Enterprise pricing, but industry reports place it at $60 to $100 per user per month depending on volume, with significant discounts for commitments of 1,000+ seats.

**What is included on Enterprise:**

- **Unlimited GPT-5 Pro access** for every user
- **Dedicated compute** — reserved GPU capacity for guaranteed performance
- **Extended 256K context window** on GPT-5 Pro
- **SAML SSO**, SCIM provisioning, and advanced IAM
- **Audit logs** and compliance reporting (SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA where applicable)
- **Custom data retention** policies
- **Dedicated customer success manager**
- **Analytics dashboard** for organization-wide usage
- **API credits** bundled into enterprise contracts

**Who Enterprise is best for:**

- Large corporations with 500+ employees using ChatGPT
- Regulated industries needing compliance reporting
- Organizations building internal tools on top of ChatGPT with heavy API usage
- Companies that need dedicated capacity to avoid rate limits during peak hours

If your organization is already spending $50,000+ per year on ChatGPT Plus or Business licenses, Enterprise often costs less after volume discounts — and delivers better guarantees.

## ChatGPT Edu and ChatGPT Gov

OpenAI also maintains two specialized plans that do not fit the standard consumer and enterprise split.

**ChatGPT Edu** is available for universities and K-12 institutions. It includes Enterprise-grade security, group admin controls, and educational pricing — typically $20 to $30 per student per year, depending on the institution size. It is the fastest-growing segment of ChatGPT in 2026 as more universities integrate AI into curricula.

**ChatGPT Gov** launched in 2025 for US federal, state, and local government agencies. It runs in Microsoft Azure Government cloud with FedRAMP High authorization, allowing agencies to deploy ChatGPT on sensitive workloads without data leaving controlled environments.

## How to choose the right ChatGPT plan

Start with these four questions.

**How often do you use ChatGPT?** If you use it a few times a week, Free is fine. Daily use pushes you to Plus. Multiple hours of daily use, especially with video generation, justifies Pro.

**Are you working alone or with a team?** Solo creators can stay on Plus or Pro. Teams of two or more benefit from Business for the shared workspace, admin controls, and data privacy guarantee. Large organizations with 500+ users should evaluate Enterprise.

**Do you need guaranteed data privacy?** Plus and Pro train on your conversations unless you opt out in settings. Business and Enterprise are no-training by default, which matters for regulated industries, legal work, and client confidentiality.

**Do you need Sora 2 video?** Free and Pro tiers differ sharply here. Plus gets a monthly quota, Pro gets effectively unlimited, and Business matches Plus. If video is core to your workflow, Pro is the only unlimited option outside Enterprise.

## What changed in 2026: the key differences from 2024-2025

For readers coming back to this after a year or two, the biggest 2026 changes are:

- **Free tier expansion.** Free now includes GPT-5 Mini and limited full GPT-5 access, matching Gemini Free competitive pressure.
- **Sora 2 inclusion.** Video generation is no longer an add-on. Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise all include Sora 2 at different quota levels.
- **Business tier rename.** The old Team plan was rebranded to Business with expanded features and a slightly higher price point.
- **Plus message caps lifted.** GPT-5 limits on Plus roughly doubled from the 2024 baseline.
- **Pro deep research mode.** Multi-step web research became a Pro-exclusive feature, positioning Pro as a research workstation.
- **Enterprise dedicated capacity.** Reserved GPUs are now available for Enterprise customers with guaranteed throughput SLAs.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is ChatGPT Plus still worth $20 per month in 2026?

Yes, for most daily users. The 2026 update added Sora 2 video, roughly doubled GPT-5 message limits, and kept the same $20 price point. If you use ChatGPT professionally more than a few times a week, Plus remains the best value tier. Casual users can stick with the expanded Free plan.

### What is the difference between ChatGPT Plus and Pro?

Plus ($20) gives you GPT-5 with expanded but capped message limits and monthly Sora 2 quotas. Pro ($200) gives you unlimited GPT-5 access, GPT-5 Pro with deeper reasoning, unlimited Sora 2, deep research mode, and early access to experimental features. Pro is worth the 10x price jump only for heavy daily users, researchers, and creators running video workflows.

### How is ChatGPT Business different from Enterprise?

Business ($25 per user) is priced for teams of 5 to 50 and includes shared workspaces, admin controls, SSO, and data privacy guarantees. Enterprise is custom-priced for organizations with 500+ users and adds dedicated compute, extended context windows, audit logs, compliance reporting, and a dedicated success manager. Most small and mid-size teams should start with Business.

### Can I use ChatGPT for free in 2026?

Yes. The free tier in 2026 includes GPT-5 Mini, limited GPT-5, standard voice, web browsing, basic image analysis, 10 DALL-E 3 images per day, and custom GPT usage. It is the most generous free tier OpenAI has ever offered. Heavy users will still hit limits, but casual users can rely on Free without needing a subscription.

### Does ChatGPT train on my data?

On Free, Plus, and Pro, ChatGPT may train on your conversations unless you opt out in privacy settings. On Business, Enterprise, Edu, and Gov tiers, training is disabled by default and your conversations are never used to improve OpenAI's models. For regulated industries, client work, or confidential research, the Business tier or higher is the safest choice.

### Which ChatGPT plan is best for SEO and content marketing?

For individual SEO specialists and content marketers, Plus at $20 is the sweet spot — enough GPT-5 access, Sora 2 for visuals, and custom GPTs for prompt automation. For agencies and in-house teams of 3+, Business at $25 per user adds shared workspaces and data privacy. Pro is only worth it if you use deep research mode daily for competitive audits and content intelligence.
