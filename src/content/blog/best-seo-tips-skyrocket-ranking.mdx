I have read hundreds of "SEO tips" articles over the years. Most of them tell you to write good content and use keywords in your headings. That advice was fine in 2018. It is not wrong in 2026, but it is incomplete to the point of being useless.

The search landscape changed. Google's AI Overviews now appear on half of all US searches. ChatGPT handles 900 million weekly queries. 65 percent of searches end without a click. The tips that actually move the needle in this environment are different from what most lists recommend.

These are the ten SEO tips that are working right now. Not theory. Not predictions. Things I have seen produce measurable results across real websites in the past twelve months.

## 1. Build topic clusters, not isolated pages

This is the single highest-impact SEO strategy available to any website in 2026. Instead of writing individual pages targeting individual keywords, build interconnected clusters of content around core topics.

A topic cluster has three parts. A pillar page that covers the broad topic comprehensively. Supporting cluster pages that go deep on specific subtopics. And internal links that connect everything together.

Here is why this works better than isolated pages: Google and AI systems evaluate topical authority, not just page quality. A site with 20 interlinked articles about [schema markup](/seo/schema-markup/) signals deeper expertise than a site with one article, regardless of how good that single article is. The cluster creates compounding authority where each new article strengthens every other article in the group.

Start with your most important topic. Map out 15 to 25 subtopics. Write the pillar page first, then build out the cluster over 4 to 8 weeks. The ranking improvements typically appear after the cluster reaches critical mass, around 10 to 12 published pages.

## 2. Write for AI extraction, not just human reading

Every piece of content you publish is now read by two audiences: humans and machines. The humans want engaging, useful content. The machines want structured, extractable answers.

The good news is that writing for both is not contradictory. It just requires a specific structure.

Start every section with a direct answer. If your H2 heading asks "What is semantic SEO?" the first sentence after it should answer that question directly: "Semantic SEO is the practice of creating content that helps search engines understand meaning, not just keywords." Then expand with context, examples, and depth.

This explain-first pattern means AI systems can extract your answer for AI Overviews, featured snippets, and ChatGPT citations, while human readers get the full context they need. A page structured this way serves both audiences without compromise.

Use clear heading hierarchy. H1 for the page title, H2 for major sections, H3 for subsections. Never skip levels. AI systems use your heading structure to understand content organization.

For the technical details, see our chapter on [on-page SEO and content optimization](/seo/on-page-seo/).

## 3. Implement schema markup on every important page

[Schema markup](/seo/schema-markup/) is the most direct way to communicate with both Google and AI systems. It is also the most underutilized SEO tactic on the web. The majority of websites still have no schema beyond what their CMS adds by default.

At minimum, every page on your site should have Article schema with author, publisher, date, and headline. Beyond that, the schemas that produce the most impact are FAQ schema on pages with question-and-answer sections, HowTo schema on tutorial and process pages, BreadcrumbList schema for navigation hierarchy, and Organization schema on your homepage.

Schema stacking, using multiple complementary schema types on a single page, creates a rich data layer that AI systems can parse from multiple angles. A page with Article plus FAQ plus BreadcrumbList gives AI systems three different ways to understand and extract your content.

The investment is small. The impact on AI visibility is disproportionately large. If you do nothing else from this list, implement schema.

## 4. Make your site accessible to AI crawlers

Google is not the only bot that matters anymore. [AI systems operate their own crawlers](/seo/technical-seo/): GPTBot for ChatGPT, ClaudeBot for Claude, PerplexityBot for Perplexity. If your robots.txt blocks these crawlers, your content is invisible to the fastest-growing search surfaces.

Check your robots.txt right now. Many sites accidentally block AI crawlers while allowing Googlebot. The fix takes 30 seconds but the impact on AI visibility is immediate.

Beyond robots.txt, consider implementing ai.txt and llms.txt. These newer standards give you granular control over how AI systems use your content. You can allow citation (being referenced in answers) while blocking training use (your content being used to train future AI models). This is the balance most publishers adopt in 2026.

For the complete technical guide, see our chapter on [LLM crawler optimization](/blog/llm-crawler-optimization/).

## 5. Prioritize E-E-A-T signals on every page

[E-E-A-T](/seo/eeat/), Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness, is no longer an abstract quality guideline. It is the primary signal that determines whether AI systems trust and cite your content.

The practical steps are straightforward. Use real author names with credentials, not "Admin" or "Staff Writer." Create author bio pages that link to published work, LinkedIn profiles, and verifiable expertise. Cite your sources with links to primary data. Include dates on statistics so readers and machines know the information is current.

For content about health, finance, legal, or safety topics (what Google calls YMYL, Your Money or Your Life), E-E-A-T signals are not optional. AI systems apply extra scrutiny to these topics and will skip sources that lack clear expertise signals.

The compounding effect matters here. Every page you publish with strong E-E-A-T signals reinforces the trust profile of your entire site. Weak E-E-A-T on one page can undermine trust across all of them.

## 6. Target questions, not just keywords

Traditional keyword research focuses on search volume and difficulty. That model misses a growing share of how people actually find information in 2026.

AI systems answer questions. Users interact with ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews through natural language questions, not two-word keyword fragments. Your content strategy should target these questions directly.

Mine questions from Reddit threads in your niche. Read YouTube comments on relevant videos. Check Google's "People Also Ask" boxes for your target topics. Ask ChatGPT and Perplexity questions in your niche and note where they struggle to give good answers, those gaps are your opportunity.

The most valuable questions are the ones AI systems currently answer poorly. If ChatGPT gives a vague or generic response to a question in your expertise area, creating the definitive answer gives you a high probability of being cited once the AI system updates its knowledge.

For the full methodology, see our guide on [AI keyword research from Reddit, YouTube, and forums](/blog/ai-keyword-research-reddit-youtube-forums/).

## 7. Optimize images with descriptive alt text and modern formats

Image SEO is one of the most neglected areas on most websites, and it matters more in 2026 than ever before. AI systems with vision capabilities like Claude Opus 4.7 now evaluate images at 3.75 megapixels. They read your alt text, analyze your image content, and use both to assess page quality.

Every image should have alt text that describes what the image shows and what it means in context. Not "photo.jpg" or "image." Descriptive, keyword-relevant alt text like "comparison table showing ChatGPT vs Claude pricing and features in 2026."

Use WebP or AVIF format for all images. These modern formats reduce file size by 25 to 50 percent versus JPEG and PNG without visible quality loss. Specify width and height attributes to prevent layout shift. Lazy load images below the fold and eagerly load the hero image.

These are small changes individually. Together they improve page speed, accessibility, [AI visibility](/blog/multimodal-seo-images-video-audio/), and user experience simultaneously.

## 8. Build an omnichannel presence beyond your website

Your website is the foundation, but AI systems evaluate your brand across every platform they can access. LinkedIn posts, YouTube videos, Reddit comments, podcast transcripts, review site profiles, all of these contribute to your entity authority.

Domains with significant brand mentions on Reddit and community platforms have roughly four times higher chances of being cited by AI systems compared to those with minimal community activity. YouTube has overtaken Reddit as the most frequently cited social platform in AI responses.

The strategy is not to be everywhere. It is to be consistently authoritative on the platforms where your audience and AI systems look for signals. Pick 2 to 3 platforms beyond your website and publish consistently. LinkedIn for professional authority. YouTube for tutorials and depth. Reddit for genuine community engagement.

For platform-specific strategies, see our chapter on [Search Everywhere Optimization](/seo/search-everywhere/).

## 9. Update and refresh your existing content

New content gets attention, but refreshing existing content often produces faster ranking improvements. Google values freshness, and a page that was accurate in 2024 may contain outdated statistics, broken links, or superseded recommendations in 2026.

Audit your top 20 performing pages every quarter. Update statistics with current data and sources. Fix broken internal and external links. Add new sections that cover developments since the original publish date. Update the published or modified date so Google and AI systems know the content is current.

The refresh does not need to be a full rewrite. Often, updating 20 to 30 percent of a page, adding a new section, updating numbers, and improving the introduction, produces more ranking impact than publishing an entirely new page on the same topic.

For the complete refresh workflow, see our deep dive on [how to refresh old SEO content](/blog/how-to-refresh-old-seo-content/).

## 10. Measure AI visibility, not just rankings

If you are only tracking traditional rankings and organic traffic, you are measuring a shrinking slice of your total search visibility. AI citations, brand mentions in AI answers, and zero-click impressions are now legitimate metrics that reflect how your audience discovers you.

Start tracking how often your content gets cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. Monitor whether AI systems represent your brand accurately. Measure the gap between your traditional rankings and your AI citation frequency, pages that rank well but are not cited by AI need structural optimization.

Tools like Otterly, Profound, and manual testing across AI platforms are the current best practices. The measurement discipline is still maturing, but the brands that start tracking now will have a data advantage when the tools improve.

## The tip behind all the tips

If I had to distill all ten of these into one principle, it would be this: stop optimizing for one search engine and start building authority across every surface where your audience finds information.

Rankings still matter. But they are a lagging indicator of something deeper. The sites that build genuine topical authority, demonstrate expertise through structured content, and maintain visibility across multiple platforms do not just rank better. They get cited by AI, referenced by other creators, and trusted by the machines that are increasingly deciding what information reaches your audience.

The [AI SEO shift](/ai-seo-shift/) is not coming. It already happened. These ten tips are how you adapt.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the most important SEO tip for 2026?

Building topic clusters is the single highest-impact SEO strategy in 2026. Instead of creating isolated pages targeting individual keywords, build interconnected content clusters around core topics. Sites with 15 to 25 interlinked articles on a topic consistently outrank competitors with single pages, because both Google and AI systems evaluate topical depth as an authority signal.

### How long does it take for SEO to work?

For long-tail keywords within a well-structured topic cluster, expect ranking improvements within 4 to 8 weeks. For medium-competition keywords, 3 to 6 months. For competitive head terms, 6 to 12 months. The compounding effect of topic clusters means results accelerate over time as each new article strengthens the entire cluster.

### Does SEO still work with AI search?

Yes, but SEO has expanded beyond traditional rankings. In 2026, effective SEO means optimizing for Google's organic results, AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and other AI search surfaces simultaneously. The tactics that work, schema markup, E-E-A-T signals, structured content, topical authority, improve visibility across all of these surfaces.

### What is the difference between SEO and AEO?

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) focuses on ranking pages in search engine results. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) focuses on getting your content selected as the source for direct answers in AI-generated responses, featured snippets, and AI Overviews. Both matter in 2026, and the most effective strategies address both simultaneously.

### How do I check if AI systems cite my content?

Test manually by asking ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Claude questions in your niche and checking if your content appears in their responses. For systematic tracking, tools like Otterly and Profound monitor AI citations at scale. Google Search Console shows impressions from AI Overviews alongside traditional search data.
