## Section 1: Categories

Primary category: Dentist (for solo practices) or Dental Clinic (for multi-provider).

Secondary categories, add every one that genuinely fits:

- Cosmetic Dentist
- Pediatric Dentist
- Orthodontist (if you offer Invisalign or braces)
- Endodontist (if you offer root canals)
- Periodontist (if you offer gum surgery)
- Oral Surgeon (if you offer surgical extractions or implants)
- Emergency Dental Service (if you take emergencies)
- Dental Implant Periodontist (if you place implants)

Mismatch between categories and actual services gets the profile demoted. Be honest.

## Section 2: Business description

You get 750 characters. Most dental practices use 200. Use them all.

Strong descriptions include:

- Years in practice and dentist credentials
- Procedures and specialties (specific, not "general dentistry")
- Insurance accepted (named carriers)
- Technology (specific equipment, CEREC, cone beam CT, laser)
- Same-day appointment availability
- Sedation options
- Multi-language services if applicable
- New patient promotions

Avoid generic phrases like "providing gentle dental care." AI engines extract distinctive content.

## Section 3: Services

The services section is criminally underused. List every distinct procedure:

- Dental cleanings and exams
- Dental implants
- Crowns and bridges
- Root canal therapy
- Cosmetic dentistry (whitening, veneers)
- Invisalign and clear aligners
- Wisdom teeth removal
- Emergency dental care
- Pediatric dentistry
- Sedation dentistry
- Periodontal care
- Oral cancer screening

Each service gets 50 to 80 words of description. AI engines extract these directly when matching patient queries.

## Section 4: Insurance

Add insurance carriers as attributes if available, and include the major ones in your business description. Insurance is one of the top three filters patients apply when choosing a dental practice.

If you do not accept insurance, surface that with "Fee-for-service practice" or "Membership plan available" language. Patients searching for fee-for-service practices specifically will find you.

## Section 5: Hours including emergency availability

If you offer emergency or after-hours appointments, use the "more hours" feature to surface them. Same-day appointment availability is one of the strongest conversion signals in the GBP panel.

## Section 6: Photos

Dental GBP photos drive both local pack visibility and conversion. Upload:

- Office exterior with signage
- Reception and waiting room
- Operatory/treatment rooms
- Equipment (especially advanced equipment, CEREC, cone beam, laser)
- Dentist and team photos
- Before-and-after of cosmetic cases (with patient consent and HIPAA compliance)

30 to 60 photos initially, refresh with 5 to 10 per month.

## Section 7: Posts

Three post types that work:

1. **Procedure spotlights** linking to your procedure pages
2. **Patient education** (oral hygiene tips, post-op care)
3. **New patient promotions** (cleaning specials, free consultations)

Cadence: one to two posts per week.

## Section 8: Q&A

Pre-empt common patient questions:

- Do you accept my insurance?
- Do you offer payment plans?
- Do you see children?
- Do you offer sedation dentistry?
- How quickly can I get an appointment?
- Do you offer same-day emergency care?
- What is the cost of a new patient exam?

Seeded Q&A populates the GBP panel and feeds AI Overviews.

## Section 9: Attributes

Dental-relevant attributes that get overlooked:

- Accepts new patients
- Online appointments
- Onsite parking
- Wheelchair accessible entrance
- LGBTQ+ friendly
- Identifies as women-owned
- Free Wi-Fi
- Languages spoken

Check every attribute that genuinely applies.

## FAQs

### Can multi-provider practices have a single GBP?

Yes, if they operate as one business at one address. Individual dentists at the practice can have personal bio pages on the website with `Person` schema, but the GBP belongs to the practice.

### What about multiple office locations?

One GBP per physical location with that location's address, phone, and reviews. Trying to run one GBP for multiple locations gets suspended.

### How do I handle HIPAA in GBP responses?

Never confirm someone is a patient. Never share treatment details. Standard response templates work around this. See the [patient reviews post](/blog/patient-reviews-dental-seo-velocity/) for templates.

### Should before-and-after photos be on GBP?

Yes, with explicit patient consent. Before-and-after cases are powerful for cosmetic dentistry visibility. Without consent, do not post.
