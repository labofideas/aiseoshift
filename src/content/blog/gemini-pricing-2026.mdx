Google sells Gemini several ways: a developer API with multiple model tiers, and consumer Google AI plans. This guide pulls the 2026 numbers together.

Last checked: September 8, 2026, against Google's official pricing pages. Confirm before budgeting; the price map has changed twice since Google I/O 2026.

![Gemini output price per 1M tokens by model](/images/posts/gemini-pricing-2026-infographic.webp)

## Gemini API pricing by model

Rates are per million tokens, input/output, standard tier.

| Model | Input ($/M) | Output ($/M) | Free tier | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gemini 3.8 Flash | $0.75 | $3.75 | Yes | Newest; promo price through Dec 31, 2026 |
| Gemini 3.7 Flash | $0.75 | $3.75 | Yes | Batch: $0.375/$1.875 |
| Gemini 3.6 Flash | $0.75 | $3.75 | Yes | |
| Gemini 3.5 Flash | $1.50 | $9.00 | Yes | Prior Flash generation |
| Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite | $0.30 | $2.50 | Yes | |
| Gemini 3.1 Pro (preview) | $2.00 | $12.00 | No | Higher rates above 200K tokens |
| Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite | $0.10 | $0.40 | Yes | Cheapest paid Gemini |

The story since June: Google shipped **three new Flash generations (3.6, 3.7, 3.8), all priced at $0.75/$3.75, half the price of Gemini 3.5 Flash.** The rate is explicitly promotional "through December 31, 2026," so budget for a possible increase in January.

One correction worth flagging: the $0.10/$0.40 floor belongs to **2.5** Flash-Lite. The 3.5 generation's Flash-Lite is listed at $0.30/$2.50. And the old "Gemini 3 Flash at $0.50/$3.00" row no longer appears on Google's pricing page at all.

**The free API tier remains the most generous in the industry.** Nearly every Flash model can be called at $0, rate-limited, if you accept that Google may use the content to improve its products. For prototyping, nothing from OpenAI, Anthropic, or xAI matches it. Note the tiered pricing on Gemini 3.1 Pro: prompts above 200K tokens cost more, so long-context work needs budgeting.

## Google AI consumer plans

| Plan | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Gemini 3.6 Flash, limited 3.1 Pro access |
| Google AI Plus | $4.99/mo | Price cut from $7.99 in June 2026; 2x usage, 400 GB storage |
| Google AI Pro | $19.99/mo | 4x usage, 5 TB, YouTube Premium Lite, $10/mo Cloud credits |
| Google AI Ultra (5x) | $100/mo | 20 TB storage |
| Google AI Ultra (20x) | $200/mo | Cut from $250 |

Google confirmed the two Ultra tiers at $100 and $200 flat on its own blog, and cut AI Plus to $4.99 while doubling its storage to 400 GB (reported by Engadget and TechCrunch when the June change shipped). At five dollars, Plus is aimed squarely at ChatGPT Go.

## Which Gemini option should you pick?

For developers, default to the new 3.6-3.8 Flash generation at $0.75/$3.75 for most work, drop to 2.5 Flash-Lite for high-volume simple tasks, and reserve Gemini 3.1 Pro for jobs that need its reasoning, watching the 200K-token price step. For consumers, AI Pro covers most needs; Plus at $4.99 is the cheapest paid AI subscription from any major provider.
