Google sells Gemini several ways: a developer API with multiple model tiers, and consumer Google AI plans. This guide pulls the 2026 numbers together.

Everything here is current as of June 10, 2026. Confirm against Google's official pages before budgeting; the price map changed notably at Google I/O 2026.

## Gemini API pricing by model

Rates are per million tokens, input/output.

| Model | Input ($/M) | Output ($/M) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gemini 3.5 Flash | $1.50 | $9.00 | Latest Flash (May 2026); strong price-performance |
| Gemini 3.1 Pro | $2.00 | $12.00 | $4/$18 above 200K tokens |
| Gemini 3 Flash | $0.50 | $3.00 | Budget workhorse |
| Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite | $0.10 | $0.40 | Cheapest; high-volume tasks |

The family follows a consistent shape: **Pro** for capability, **Flash** for speed and balance, and **Flash-Lite** for cost efficiency, plus specialized variants for computer use, deep reasoning, and image generation. Note the tiered pricing on Gemini 3.1 Pro: prompts above 200K tokens cost more, so long-context work needs budgeting.

Gemini's strengths are price-performance at the Flash tiers and deep integration with Google Workspace and tools. Gemini 3.5 Flash in particular is a strong pick for bulk content work, and Gemini 3.1 Pro scores well on science benchmarks (around 94% GPQA).

## Google AI consumer plans

| Plan | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Google AI Plus | ~$7.99/mo | Entry consumer tier |
| Google AI Pro | ~$19.99/mo | Flagship access for individuals |
| Google AI Ultra | ~$99.99/mo (entry) | Repriced in 2026; ~5x Pro limits, 20 TB storage |
| Google AI Ultra (top) | ~$200/mo | Up to 20x Pro limits, full tool and video suite |

Google cut Ultra pricing sharply in 2026, so the top tier that once sat at around $250/month is now lower. Verify the current tiers, since Google has been adjusting them.

## Which Gemini option should you pick?

For developers, default to Gemini 3 Flash or 3.5 Flash for most work, drop to Flash-Lite for high-volume simple tasks, and reserve Gemini 3.1 Pro for jobs that need its reasoning, watching the 200K-token price step. For consumers, AI Pro covers most needs, and Ultra is for power users who want the full Google tool and video suite.
