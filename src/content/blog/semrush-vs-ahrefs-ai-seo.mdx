If you are running an AI SEO program in 2026 and you have not yet made a decision between Semrush and Ahrefs, you are in good company. Both tools have expanded their feature sets aggressively over the past eighteen months, and the gap between them on traditional SEO tasks has narrowed considerably. The divergence has happened on AI-specific features, and that divergence is where this comparison focuses.

The short answer: Semrush has moved faster and more deliberately on AI visibility and AI Overview monitoring. Ahrefs has the cleaner interface, the stronger backlink index by most independent benchmarks, and an increasingly capable AI-era keyword suite. Neither tool does everything well. Which one is worth the money depends entirely on where your AI SEO work is concentrated.

For teams who want the full breakdown on how these tools fit into a broader AI SEO stack, the [AI SEO platform comparison for 2026](/blog/ai-seo-platform-comparison-2026/) gives the wider context. This post goes deep on the head-to-head.

## Quick verdict by use case

Before going feature by feature, here is the summary for the use cases that matter most in 2026:

- **AI Overview monitoring:** Semrush, and it is not close
- **Conversational keyword research:** Semrush, but Ahrefs is catching up fast
- **Content gap analysis for AI-vulnerable topics:** Ahrefs, especially for finding zero-traffic competitor pages
- **Backlink analysis and link authority signals:** Ahrefs, still the gold standard
- **Content optimization and on-page guidance:** Semrush, by a meaningful margin
- **Pricing and value at entry-level:** Ahrefs, if you can live without AI Toolkit features

Keep this in mind as you read through each section. The tool that wins for your team depends on which of these use cases dominates your workflow.

## Keyword research: conversational queries and AI intent

The fundamental shift in keyword research for AI SEO is the move from individual keyword terms to intent clusters, and from search-bar queries to conversational questions. Both tools handle this differently.

[Semrush](https://semrush.com) has leaned into conversational intent with its Keyword Magic Tool and Topic Research module. The Keyword Magic Tool surfaces question-format queries explicitly, filters by intent type, and groups results by semantic clusters out of the box. The prompt database that powers the AI Toolkit, 239 million real user prompts, also feeds keyword research in ways that Ahrefs cannot match yet. If you are trying to find the exact question phrasings that trigger Google AI Overviews in your niche, Semrush has the data infrastructure to answer that question.

[Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com) matches Semrush on raw keyword volume and difficulty data. Its Questions filter in Keywords Explorer is genuinely useful and pulls from a very large index. The difference is in what the tool does after surfacing those questions. Ahrefs gives you the data cleanly. It is less opinionated about how to act on it. For teams with experienced analysts who know how to interpret keyword data without guardrails, that is a feature. For teams newer to AI keyword research, the Semrush workflow is more instructive.

For a broader look at [keyword research tools for AI SEO](/blog/best-ai-keyword-research-tools/), including tools that go beyond either platform, that guide covers the full landscape. But for teams choosing between these two as their primary research platform, the edge goes to Semrush for AI-intent keyword work specifically.

One practical note: Ahrefs' "Also rank for" and "Traffic Share" features remain excellent for finding the full topical footprint around a seed keyword. Neither tool is useless for this work. Semrush just has more AI-specific data layers.

## Content gap analysis: finding AI-vulnerable opportunities

Content gap analysis in the AI era has a new dimension. You are not just looking for keywords your competitors rank for that you do not. You are looking for the question clusters where AI Overviews are displacing everyone's organic traffic, and where being the cited source in the AI answer matters more than ranking number one below it.

This is where Ahrefs genuinely excels. Its Content Gap tool is more precise and more actionable than Semrush's equivalent. The ability to find competitor pages that attract zero organic clicks despite solid rankings, a signal of AI Overview displacement, is cleaner in Ahrefs. You can identify content that competitors invested in that is now underperforming because AI Overviews have captured the intent, and those are exactly the topics where high-quality, citation-worthy content can win.

Semrush's equivalent tooling (Keyword Gap and the Competitive Research module) is solid but feels slightly more cluttered. The AI-specific filter options are better in Semrush, but the underlying content gap workflow is smoother in Ahrefs.

For AI SEO specifically, content gap analysis is about finding two types of opportunity: topics your competitors cover where AI Overviews cite them instead of you, and topics where no competitor has published content that satisfies the AI's standards for citation. Both tools can surface these gaps, but Ahrefs gets you there with fewer clicks.

The [AI SEO audit checklist](/blog/ai-seo-audit-checklist/) includes a content gap audit step that works with either tool's data. Running that audit is worth doing regardless of which platform you choose.

## Backlink tracking and AI authority signals

Link authority still matters for AI SEO, but the mechanism has shifted. AI systems do not read PageRank scores directly. What they do respond to is the breadth and quality of the topical authority signals that links create, which publishers cite you, which experts link to your content, whether your brand appears in authoritative contexts across the web.

For measuring all of this, Ahrefs remains the cleaner choice. Its link index is consistently rated the largest and most accurate by independent audits. The referring domains filter, the anchor text analysis, and the historical link velocity data in Ahrefs are simply better than Semrush's equivalent features in most benchmark comparisons.

Semrush has closed the gap over the past two years. Its backlink database is meaningfully larger than it was, and its Link Building Tool and Backlink Audit are fully functional. But if backlink analysis is a significant portion of your AI SEO workflow, particularly if you are doing digital PR to build topical authority that AI systems can recognize, Ahrefs is the stronger tool.

The nuance worth noting for AI SEO: the quality dimension of backlinks matters more than quantity. A single link from a major publication in your industry does more for your AI citation probability than dozens of links from tangentially related sites. Both tools let you filter by domain authority and topical relevance, but Ahrefs' interface for doing that analysis is more intuitive.

For teams doing link building specifically to improve AI visibility, the relationship between backlinks and AI authority signals is covered in depth in the guide on [how to get cited by AI search systems](/blog/how-to-get-cited-by-ai-search-systems/).

## AI Overviews monitoring: which tool actually tracks it

This is the category with the widest gap between the two platforms, and it favors Semrush clearly.

Semrush built its AI Toolkit explicitly to monitor AI Overviews and other AI search surfaces. It tracks ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, [Google AI Mode](/ai-mode-seo/), Perplexity, and Gemini. The AI Visibility Score compares your brand's citation frequency against competitors. The prompt database surfaces the exact queries triggering AI responses in your niche. The AI Search Site Audit connects visibility gaps to specific content and technical fixes.

Ahrefs added SERP feature tracking for AI Overviews, you can see whether a keyword triggers an AI Overview in the SERP, but it does not track citation data. You can see that an AI Overview appears for a query. You cannot see who is cited in it, how often your brand appears, or how your AI visibility compares to competitors over time.

That is a fundamental difference. Semrush's AI Toolkit gives you a measurement framework for AI visibility. Ahrefs' current offering gives you SERP feature indicators. For teams where AI Overview monitoring is a reporting requirement, Semrush is the only choice of the two.

The [AEO KPIs and metrics for AI visibility](/blog/aeo-kpis-metrics-ai-visibility/) framework is worth reading alongside this comparison. The metrics it defines, citation rate, AI share of voice, zero-click impression share, are the outputs that Semrush's AI Toolkit is designed to populate. Ahrefs cannot currently produce most of those metrics from its own data.

## Content optimization: on-page AI SEO guidance

Both tools offer content optimization features, but their approaches are different.

Semrush's SEO Writing Assistant and Content Audit module are the most developed in-platform content optimization tools either company offers. The SEO Writing Assistant provides real-time suggestions for semantic coverage, readability, and entity usage as you write or paste content. For AI SEO specifically, where satisfying a broad topical intent matters more than hitting individual keyword density targets, the semantic suggestions are useful.

The Content Audit in Semrush flags pages for update, rewrite, or removal based on traffic trends and content freshness signals. In an AI SEO context, identifying pages that have lost traffic to AI Overviews without being replaced is valuable, those are the pages most in need of restructuring as citation-worthy content.

Ahrefs' content optimization tooling is less developed. The Content Explorer is an excellent tool for finding high-performing content in your space, useful for benchmarking against what gets cited, but it does not provide on-page optimization guidance. The Site Audit handles technical SEO well but does not extend into content quality signals in the way Semrush does.

For on-page optimization, Semrush is the stronger choice. For competitive content intelligence, understanding what types of content dominate in your niche, Ahrefs' Content Explorer is underrated and worth using alongside whichever platform you choose as primary.

## Pricing and value for AI-focused teams

The pricing comparison is more nuanced than it first appears, because the most important AI features in Semrush are locked behind an additional tier.

Semrush's standard plans start at $139.95 per month (Pro), $249.95 (Guru), and $499.95 (Business). The AI Toolkit, the feature set that makes Semrush genuinely competitive for AI visibility monitoring, is included with Semrush One, their bundled plan at $199 per month, or as an add-on. If you want the AI Toolkit's full feature set with the prompt database and AI Overview citation tracking, budget accordingly.

Ahrefs pricing is $129 per month (Lite), $249 (Standard), $449 (Advanced), and enterprise pricing above that. At the equivalent tier, Ahrefs typically includes more generous crawl limits and data exports. The Ahrefs team has also invested in its Ahrefs Webmaster Tools free tier, which provides meaningful data for smaller sites without a paid plan.

For an AI-focused team that needs AI Overview monitoring and citation tracking as core workflow requirements, Semrush at the relevant tier is the correct choice even though it costs more. The AI Toolkit features are not available from Ahrefs at any price point.

For a team that is primarily doing keyword research, content gap analysis, and backlink work, with AI SEO layered on top of traditional SEO rather than at the center, Ahrefs at Standard or Advanced is excellent value and the UI will cause less friction day to day.

The [best AI analytics tools for SEO](/blog/best-ai-analytics-tools-seo/) covers additional platforms that can supplement either tool for teams who need deeper analytics coverage than either Semrush or Ahrefs provides natively.

## Who should choose Semrush

Choose Semrush if your AI SEO program requires AI Overview monitoring as a deliverable. If you are reporting to stakeholders on AI visibility, AI share of voice, or citation rate, and you need that data from a recognized platform rather than manual spot-checking, Semrush is the only tool of the two that can produce it.

Semrush also wins for teams doing heavy content optimization work. The SEO Writing Assistant and the integration between keyword research, content briefs, and on-page optimization create a workflow that Ahrefs does not replicate. Agencies managing content production for multiple clients will find the Semrush content workflow more efficient.

If you are new to AI SEO and want a platform that tells you what to do, not just what the data says, Semrush is more opinionated in a useful way.

## Who should choose Ahrefs

Choose Ahrefs if backlink analysis and link building are central to your AI SEO strategy. If you are trying to build topical authority through digital PR, link acquisition, and brand mentions, which do feed into AI citation probability indirectly, Ahrefs is the stronger tool for that work.

Ahrefs also wins for teams doing deep competitive research. The Site Explorer, the Content Explorer, and the Keywords Explorer together create the best single-platform environment for understanding why a competitor performs the way it does. If your AI SEO work starts with competitive analysis and works inward to content strategy, Ahrefs surfaces that intelligence faster.

For technical SEO teams focused on crawlability, indexation, and structured data, all of which matter for AI discoverability, Ahrefs' Site Audit is cleaner and its data exports are more flexible.

The [AI SEO shift platform overview](/ai-seo-shift/) explains how traditional SEO foundations connect to AI visibility. Both tools play a role in building those foundations, and neither replaces the other entirely.

## The case for using both

It is worth saying plainly: many serious AI SEO teams use both. Ahrefs for backlink analysis, competitive research, and content gap work. Semrush for AI Overview monitoring, content optimization, and AI visibility reporting. The total cost is significant, but for agencies and in-house teams where AI SEO is a core deliverable, the overlap between the two tools is minimal enough that the combination is genuinely additive.

If budget requires choosing one, the decision comes down to the single question of whether AI Overview monitoring is a reporting requirement. If yes: Semrush. If not yet: Ahrefs gives better value per dollar on the tasks that remain.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is Semrush or Ahrefs better for AI SEO in 2026?**

Semrush is better for AI SEO if AI Overview monitoring and citation tracking are part of your reporting requirements. Its AI Toolkit tracks ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and Gemini, with a prompt database of over 239 million queries. Ahrefs is better for backlink analysis, content gap research, and competitive intelligence, which are foundational to the authority signals that AI systems use when selecting citations.

**Does Ahrefs track Google AI Overviews?**

Ahrefs shows whether a keyword triggers a Google AI Overview in the SERP, but it does not track who is cited inside the AI Overview or measure citation frequency over time. For citation tracking and AI visibility scoring, Semrush's AI Toolkit is the only major platform of the two that provides that data.

**Can I use Semrush for keyword research for AI SEO?**

Yes. Semrush's Keyword Magic Tool and Topic Research module are well-suited to finding conversational and question-format queries that trigger AI responses. The platform's prompt database, included in the AI Toolkit, surfaces the exact question phrasings that trigger AI answers in a given niche, which goes beyond what standard keyword tools provide.

**Which tool has better backlink data, Semrush or Ahrefs?**

Ahrefs consistently ranks as the larger and more accurate backlink index in independent benchmark studies. For digital PR, link analysis, and building the topical authority signals that influence AI citation probability, Ahrefs is the stronger tool.

**Is Semrush worth the extra cost for AI SEO teams?**

For teams that need AI Overview monitoring and AI visibility reporting as deliverables, yes. The AI Toolkit features that make Semrush uniquely valuable for AI SEO are not available in any Ahrefs plan. For teams where AI SEO is supplementary to traditional SEO work, Ahrefs often provides better value per dollar.

**Can Semrush and Ahrefs be used together for AI SEO?**

Yes, and many serious AI SEO teams do exactly that. The tools complement each other: Ahrefs for backlink analysis, competitive research, and content gap identification; Semrush for AI visibility monitoring, content optimization, and AI Overview citation tracking. The overlap between the two is small enough that combining them is genuinely additive for teams that can absorb the combined cost.
