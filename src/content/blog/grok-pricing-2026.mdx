## Grok API pricing by model

Rates are per million tokens, input/output.

| Model | Input ($/M) | Output ($/M) | Context | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Grok 4.3 | $1.25 | $2.50 | 1M | Reasoning-first flagship (Apr 2026) |
| Grok 4.1 Fast | $0.20 | $0.50 | 2M | Cheapest rate, largest context |
| Grok 4 Heavy | varies | varies | 256k | Multi-agent, highest benchmarks |

**Grok 4.3** is xAI's flagship, a reasoning-first model at a strikingly low $1.25/$2.50, with cached input around $0.20. **Grok 4.1 Fast** is the budget champion of the entire market at $0.20/$0.50, and it carries a 2M-token context, the largest in this comparison. **Grok 4 Heavy** is the multi-agent, parallel-compute model with the highest in-house benchmarks, aimed at the hardest problems.

The headline takeaway: on raw price per token, Grok undercuts Claude, GPT, and Gemini across the board, which makes it compelling for cost-sensitive, high-volume workloads, especially where a very large context helps.

| X Premium+ | $40/mo | Bundles Grok with X platform perks |
| SuperGrok Heavy | $300/mo | Full access including Grok 4.3 / Heavy |
| Grok Business | $30/user/mo | Team billing, admin controls |

## Which Grok option should you pick?

For developers, Grok 4.1 Fast is hard to beat on cost for high-volume or large-context work, with Grok 4.3 when you need stronger reasoning, still at a low price. For consumers, SuperGrok at $30/month is the standard full-access tier; X Premium+ makes sense if you already want the X perks; Heavy is for power users who need the top model and the most compute.

The trade-off to weigh is capability: Grok's coding-benchmark scores trail the leaders (its SWE-bench numbers vary widely by scaffold), so if your workload is hard coding, compare against Claude and GPT before committing on price alone.
