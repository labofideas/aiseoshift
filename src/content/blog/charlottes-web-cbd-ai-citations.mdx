If you have searched CBD questions in ChatGPT or Google AI Overviews, you have seen the pattern. A few brands come up again and again. One of them is almost always Charlotte's Web, which has held the top CBD position in AI citations for years, and its lead is widening rather than shrinking.

It is tempting to read that as a closed door. It is not. But you have to understand why the incumbent wins before you can route around it.

![How smaller CBD brands compete against incumbent citation leaders](/images/posts/charlottes-web-cbd-ai-citations-infographic.webp)

## Why incumbents own the citations

A cautious engine cites the source it is most confident about. Confidence comes from signals that accumulate over time: brand recognition, third-party validation, broad and consistent coverage, a long track record of not being wrong.

Charlotte's Web has all of that. So when an engine answers a CBD question, citing them is the low-risk move. And here is the compounding part: every citation reinforces the authority that earns the next one. The lead feeds itself.

That is a real moat. Trying to out-broad it is a losing game.

## The opening incumbents leave

Breadth is the incumbent's strength. It is also where they are thin.

A brand that covers everything covers nothing deeply. Ask a narrow question, a specific format, a specific use case, a specific local need, and the generic leader often has a generic answer. That gap is your entry point.

Engines do not only cite the biggest brand. They cite the most relevant, trustworthy answer to the specific question. On a narrow question, relevance can beat size.

## How smaller brands actually compete

**Own a lane, not the category.** Pick a product, format, ingredient combination, or use case you can be the clear best answer for. Depth on one surface beats thin coverage across ten.

**Be undeniable on trust.** The incumbent's edge is trust signals. You can match them on a smaller scope: credentialed authors, prominent third-party lab results, transparent sourcing, and real, specific reviews. See how trust drives [CBD AI citation](/blog/cbd-ai-seo) in detail.

**Use local if you have it.** A physical store or clinic is something most national brands lack. Local presence is a trust and relevance signal national competitors cannot replicate, and AI local answers reward it. Our guide to [local SEO in the AI era](/blog/local-seo-2026-ai-overview-local-packs) covers the mechanics.

**Go deep on the questions the leader answers generically.** Dosing considerations for a specific situation, format comparisons, how to choose for a particular need. Claim-safe, specific, credentialed content on narrow questions is exactly what an engine reaches for when the broad answer does not fit.

## The takeaway

You will not beat Charlotte's Web by being a smaller Charlotte's Web. You beat the moat by being undeniable somewhere specific, where size does not help and depth does. Win the narrow questions first, stack the trust signals, and let the citations compound for you the way they have compounded for the incumbent.
