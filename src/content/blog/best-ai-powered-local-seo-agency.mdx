Local SEO used to mean Google My Business, local citations, and review management. That foundation still matters, but the game has expanded significantly. A growing percentage of local queries now return AI-generated answers -- from Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, and Microsoft Copilot -- before a single blue link or map result appears.

When someone in Chicago asks ChatGPT "best plumber near me" or asks Google AI Overviews "most trusted HVAC companies in Austin," the AI engine selects which businesses to mention. If your local business is not being cited in those answers, you are invisible in an interface that a significant share of your potential customers are now using.

An AI-powered local SEO agency handles both layers: the classical local signals (GMB, citations, reviews) and the AI citation layer (structured data, entity optimization, content that AI engines treat as locally authoritative).

## What makes local AI SEO different from standard local SEO

Standard local SEO targets ranking in the Google Maps pack and local organic results. AI-powered local SEO adds the citation layer -- optimizing for the answers AI engines give when users ask local questions.

The key differences in the work:

**Entity accuracy over citation volume.** Classical local SEO builds citation volume across directories. AI local SEO prioritizes accuracy and consistency of entity information across the sources AI engines trust most (Google, Bing Places, Wikidata, Yelp, industry directories).

**Conversational content for local queries.** AI engines answer "best Italian restaurant downtown Nashville" differently than Google Maps returns results for it. Creating content that directly addresses local question formats -- neighborhood guides, "best [service] in [city]" pages, local FAQ content -- improves AI citation eligibility.

**Service area schema precision.** LocalBusiness schema with `areaServed`, `geo`, and `serviceType` markup gives AI engines machine-readable confirmation of what your business does and where. Many local businesses have no structured data at all.

**Review velocity and quality.** AI engines include review signals in local authority assessments. A business with 400 reviews averaging 4.8 is more citable than one with 40 reviews at the same average. Systematic review generation strategy is part of local AI SEO.

## Questions to ask before hiring

When evaluating a local AI SEO agency, the questions that reveal actual capability:

- **Which AI engines do you track for local queries?** Google AI Overviews and AI Mode are minimum. ChatGPT and Copilot matter for many local audiences.
- **How do you implement LocalBusiness schema?** Look for specific answers about areaServed, serviceType, and opening hours markup, not just "we add schema."
- **How do you measure AI citation for local queries?** If they only report Google Maps rankings, they are not yet doing AI-era local SEO.
- **Can you show a local client whose AI citation visibility improved?** Before/after data from local AI presence monitoring is the right evidence.
