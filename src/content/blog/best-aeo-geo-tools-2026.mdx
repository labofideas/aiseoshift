A year ago, "AI visibility" was a concept discussed in strategy decks. In May 2026, it is a measurable discipline with dedicated software, established metrics, and platforms that track whether your brand gets cited when ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, or Gemini answers a question in your niche.

The category has matured fast. Enterprise platforms now track AI citations across five or more AI systems. Free tools let anyone check their brand's AI visibility in seconds. And the traditional SEO tool vendors have all shipped AI visibility features to stay relevant.

But the landscape is fragmented. Some tools track citations. Others optimize content. A few do both. Pricing ranges from free to $699 per month. And the difference between a tool that provides actionable data and one that generates vanity metrics is not always obvious.

This guide ranks the 15 best AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) and GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) tools available right now. We grouped them by function, compared features and pricing, and identified which tool fits each team size and use case.

## How we evaluated these tools

Every tool was assessed across five criteria:

- **AI platform coverage.** How many AI systems does it track? ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, Claude, Grok?
- **Actionable insights.** Does it just report citations, or does it tell you why you are or are not being cited and what to fix?
- **Competitive benchmarking.** Can you compare your AI visibility against competitors?
- **Integration with existing workflows.** Does it connect to your CMS, analytics, or existing SEO tools?
- **Pricing transparency and value.** Is the pricing clear, and does the value justify the cost at your scale?

## Best all-in-one AEO and GEO platforms

These tools combine AI visibility tracking, competitive benchmarking, and content optimization in unified platforms.

The toolkit tracks five AI platforms: ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Perplexity, and Gemini. It benchmarks your AI Visibility Score against up to nine competitors, tracking Share of Voice, sentiment, and citation frequency across all surfaces.

What sets Semrush apart is the prompt database: over 239 million real user prompts that show exactly which questions trigger AI responses in your niche. The AI Search Site Audit connects your visibility gaps to concrete technical and content fixes, not just metrics. Country filtering enables per-market AEO reporting, critical for brands operating in multiple regions.

Brand Performance tracking runs weekly across all covered AI platforms. Competitor Research benchmarks up to four rival brands per market on mentions, citations, and topic coverage. The integration with Semrush's existing keyword, content, and technical SEO tools means you can go from identifying an AI visibility gap to fixing it without leaving the platform.

- **AI platforms tracked:** ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Perplexity, Gemini

## How to choose the right AEO/GEO tool

### By team size

**Solo practitioner or freelancer:** Start with HubSpot AEO Grader (free) to assess your baseline. Add Airefs ($39/month) or Otterly ($49/month) when you are ready for ongoing tracking. Use Surfer ($89/month) for content optimization.

**Small team (2-10 people):** [Semrush](https://semrush.sjv.io/PyEbGz) AI Toolkit ($139-199/month) gives you AI visibility plus full traditional SEO in one platform. Add Otterly or Peec for deeper citation analysis if needed.

**Mid-market (10-50 people):** Profound ($399/month Growth plan) for prescriptive optimization plus Semrush or Ahrefs for traditional SEO coverage. Add Clearscope or MarketMuse for content workflow integration.

**Enterprise (50+ people):** Profound (Enterprise) or BrightEdge for AI visibility and entity management. Ahrefs Brand Radar ($699/month) for comprehensive AI traffic attribution. Semrush for per-market reporting across regions.

### By primary need

**"I just want to know if AI cites me":** HubSpot AEO Grader (free), then Otterly or Peec for ongoing monitoring.

**"I want to improve my AI citations":** [Semrush](https://semrush.sjv.io/PyEbGz) AI Toolkit (identifies gaps + provides fixes) or Profound (prescriptive optimization).

**"I need to optimize content for AI extraction":** Surfer for content editing, MarketMuse for content planning, Schema Pro for structured data.

**"I need to track competitors' AI visibility":** Otterly (citation analysis), [Semrush](https://semrush.sjv.io/PyEbGz) (benchmarks 9 competitors), Profound (competitive displacement).

## What AEO and GEO tools cannot do

No tool replaces the fundamentals. AEO/GEO platforms track and optimize your AI visibility, but the underlying authority still comes from content quality, [E-E-A-T signals](/seo/eeat/), topical depth, and the kind of genuine expertise that machines can detect but not manufacture.

If your content is thin, your site has no schema markup, and your brand has zero presence outside your website, no AEO tool will make AI systems cite you. These tools optimize the signal. You have to build the substance.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best AEO tool in 2026?

Semrush AI Toolkit is the best all-in-one AEO tool for most teams because it integrates AI visibility tracking with the full traditional SEO toolkit you already need. For dedicated AEO tracking without a full SEO suite, Profound is the enterprise leader and Otterly.AI provides the deepest citation analysis at mid-market pricing.

### What is the difference between AEO tools and GEO tools?

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) tools primarily track and improve your brand's visibility in AI-generated answers. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) tools focus on optimizing content specifically for generative AI consumption. In practice, the categories overlap significantly. Most platforms marketed as "AEO" or "GEO" tools do both tracking and optimization.

### Are there free AEO tools?

Yes. HubSpot AEO Grader provides a free instant AI visibility assessment with no signup required. MarketMuse offers a free tier for content strategy. AISEOShift provides free browser-based SEO structure checks. For ongoing monitoring, paid tools start at $39 per month (Airefs) and $49 per month (Otterly, Scrunch).

### How much do AEO tools cost?

Pricing ranges from free (HubSpot AEO Grader) to $699 per month (Ahrefs Brand Radar full add-on). Most teams spend $49 to $199 per month on AI visibility tooling. Enterprise platforms like Profound and BrightEdge start at $399 per month and scale to custom pricing. Semrush AI Toolkit at $139 to $199 per month offers the best value for teams that also need traditional SEO tools.

### Do I need an AEO tool if I already use Semrush or Ahrefs?

If you use Semrush, the AI Toolkit adds comprehensive AEO capabilities within your existing platform at no extra cost on Pro plans and above. If you use Ahrefs, Brand Radar provides AI visibility tracking, though the full AI indexes add $699 per month. Both options avoid the need for a separate dedicated AEO tool for most use cases.

### How do I know if AEO tools are working?

Track three metrics over time: AI citation frequency (how often your brand appears in AI responses), citation accuracy (whether AI systems describe your brand correctly), and AI-driven traffic (visits from ChatGPT, Perplexity, and other AI referral sources). Most AEO platforms report these metrics weekly. Meaningful improvements typically appear within 4 to 8 weeks of implementing recommended changes.
