The best ChatGPT rank trackers in 2026 are SE Visible for most teams, Otterly.ai for the smallest budgets, Peec AI for agencies, and Profound for enterprise brands. A ChatGPT rank tracker runs your buyers' questions through ChatGPT on a schedule and records whether ChatGPT mentioned your brand, whether it cited one of your pages as a source, where in the answer you appeared, and which competitors it picked instead. There is no position 1 to 10. ChatGPT writes one answer, so the numbers that matter are mention rate, citation rate and share of voice.

ChatGPT is the engine to get right first. OpenAI said at DevDay on September 29 that ChatGPT now has 1.2 billion weekly users, up from 900 million in February.

That is a lot of people asking it what to buy.

## Why ChatGPT is harder to track than Google

Google gives everyone roughly the same ten links. ChatGPT does not, for four reasons.

**Memory.** Logged-in users with memory or chat history get answers shaped by what ChatGPT knows about them. Two people asking the same question can get different brand recommendations.

**Search or no search.** Some questions trigger a live web search, and the answer comes with citations. Others are answered from training data with no sources at all. The same question can go either way on different days.

**Location.** Local and regional questions change with where the user is.

**The model itself.** Answers are sampled, so the wording and the shortlist shift between runs, and a model update can move everything at once.

A tracker that runs each prompt once a week, through an API, from one location, is measuring noise. The good ones run prompts daily, repeat them, control location, and report rates over time.

## What a ChatGPT tracker should measure

**Mention rate.** The share of runs where your brand appears. This is your baseline.

**Citation rate.** The share of runs where ChatGPT links to one of your pages. Citations only happen when search kicks in, so this number is smaller and more valuable.

**Position.** Whether you are the first recommendation or the fifth. ChatGPT users rarely act on the fifth.

**Cited sources.** The full list of URLs ChatGPT relied on for your prompts, including competitors' and third-party pages. This is the most useful output of any tracker, because it tells you which pages to beat and which publications to get into.

**Share of voice.** Your mentions against named competitors across the whole prompt set.

**Sentiment.** Whether ChatGPT recommends you, mentions you neutrally, or adds a caveat. A mention with "users report billing problems" is not a win.

## API data vs real browser data

This is the question most buyers skip, and it matters more for ChatGPT than any other engine.

Some tools query OpenAI's models through the API. That is cheap and fast, but the API is not the ChatGPT app. It does not carry the app's search behavior, shopping cards or interface in the same way, so API answers can differ from what your customers actually see.

Other tools load ChatGPT in a real browser, ask the question the way a person would, and record the answer and its sources. That costs more to run and is closer to reality. SE Visible states that it collects answers this way. Otterly.ai describes sending real queries to the engines. Trakkr describes its data points as single queries to the model. If a vendor does not say how it collects ChatGPT answers, ask before you buy.

## How to track ChatGPT for free

If you are not ready to pay, a manual baseline takes an afternoon.

1. Write 20 to 30 questions your buyers actually ask, in their words. Mix "best X for Y" questions with "X vs Y" and "how do I" questions.
2. Open ChatGPT in a private window, logged out, so memory does not skew results.
3. Run each question three times. Note whether your brand appears, where, whether you are cited, and who else is named.
4. Log it in a spreadsheet with the date. Repeat monthly.

It is slow and the sample is small, but it gives you a real baseline to compare any paid tool against. Our guide to [how to track AI citations](/blog/how-to-track-ai-citations/) includes a ready-made tracking sheet.

## Turning ChatGPT tracking data into more mentions

The tracker's job ends at the cited-sources list. Yours starts there.

When ChatGPT searches, it draws on Bing's index, so check where your key pages rank on Bing, not just Google. Our breakdown of [how ChatGPT search selects its sources](/blog/how-chatgpt-search-selects-sources/) covers the patterns. Then look at the third-party pages ChatGPT keeps citing for your category: review sites, comparison articles, Reddit threads. Getting your brand onto those pages often moves ChatGPT faster than editing your own site.

For answers ChatGPT gives without searching, the lever is your brand's footprint across the web over time. That is slower work, covered in [how to optimize for ChatGPT search](/blog/how-to-optimize-for-chatgpt-search/).

## Common ChatGPT tracking mistakes

**Tracking logged in.** Your own chat history shapes the answers you see. Always test logged out, or let the tool do it.

**Reading single days.** Answers vary run to run. Judge trends over weeks.

**Tracking your own brand name.** "What is [your brand]" will almost always mention you. Track the category questions buyers ask before they know you exist.

**Ignoring sentiment.** A rising mention rate means nothing if ChatGPT keeps attaching a warning to your name.
