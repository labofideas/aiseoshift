The AI meeting note taker category matured fast. In 2024, these tools could barely keep up with fast talkers. In 2026, the best ones transcribe in real time with 95 percent accuracy, generate structured summaries seconds after the call ends, extract action items with owner assignments, and push everything to your CRM without you touching a keyboard.

But the market also fractured. Some tools send a bot into your meeting that announces itself to every participant. Others record silently from your device. Some integrate with every platform. Others only work on Zoom. The pricing models range from completely free to $50 per user per month — and the free tiers vary wildly in what they actually include.

One important development in 2026: Google rolled out an update in March that places third-party meeting bots in a "potential risk" queue, defaulting to deny their entry on Google Meet. This forced a split in the market between bot-based tools and bot-free alternatives that record locally from your device.

This guide ranks the 20 best AI meeting note takers available right now. We grouped them by approach, compared free tiers and paid pricing, and identified which app fits each type of user — from solo freelancers to enterprise sales teams.

## How we evaluated these tools

Every tool was assessed across six criteria:

- **Transcription accuracy.** How well does it handle fast speech, accents, technical jargon, and multiple speakers?
- **Summary quality.** Are the AI summaries actually useful, or generic fluff you rewrite anyway?
- **Action item extraction.** Does it correctly identify commitments, deadlines, and owners?
- **Platform support.** Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and other platforms.
- **Bot vs bot-free.** Does it send a visible bot into your meeting, or record locally?
- **Pricing and free tier.** What do you actually get for free, and what does the first paid tier cost?

## Best overall AI meeting note takers

These tools handle the full meeting workflow — transcription, summaries, action items, and integrations — for most use cases.

### 1. Fathom — Best free AI note taker

[Fathom](https://fathom.video/) is the best overall AI meeting note taker because it offers the most generous free tier in the market: unlimited recordings with five AI summaries per month, no credit card required. The paid tier unlocks unlimited AI summaries for roughly $15 to $29 per user per month.

Post-call processing takes approximately 30 seconds — among the fastest in the category. Transcription accuracy sits at 95 percent. The tool integrates with Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams, and pushes meeting notes to Slack, HubSpot, Salesforce, and Notion.

Fathom holds a 5.0 out of 5 rating on G2 from over 6,000 reviews — the highest-rated tool in this entire category.

- **Platforms:** Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams
- **Key features:** Unlimited free recordings, 30-second processing, CRM integration, highlight clips, AI summaries
- **Pricing:** Free (unlimited recordings, 5 AI summaries/month). Premium $15-$29/user/month
- **Bot or bot-free:** Bot-based (joins as participant)
- **Best for:** Solo users and small teams who want the best free option with an upgrade path

### 2. tl;dv — Best for teams with customizable notes

[tl;dv](https://tldv.io/) offers the most customizable AI meeting notes in the category. You can define templates for different meeting types — sales calls, standup notes, customer interviews — and the AI structures its output to match your format.

The killer feature: tl;dv offers both a bot recorder and a native desktop app that records without sending a bot into the call. This sidesteps Google's March 2026 bot restrictions entirely. The desktop app captures Google Meet sessions locally with no bot in the participant list and no host approval needed.

- **Platforms:** Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams
- **Key features:** Custom note templates, bot-free desktop app, highlight moments, team workspace, CRM integration
- **Pricing:** Free (unlimited recordings and transcripts). Pro $18/user/month, Business $59/user/month
- **Bot or bot-free:** Both options (bot recorder + native desktop app)
- **Best for:** Teams that need structured, templated meeting documentation across different meeting types

### 3. Fireflies — Best for global teams

[Fireflies](https://fireflies.ai/) leads on language support with transcription in 100+ languages — critical for international teams with multilingual meetings. The AI generates summaries, action items, and searchable transcripts with speaker identification.

Fireflies also offers a unique "Threads" feature where team members can comment on specific parts of a transcript, creating async discussion around meeting moments.

- **Platforms:** Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex, GoToMeeting, and more
- **Key features:** 100+ language support, searchable transcripts, Threads commenting, topic detection, sentiment analysis
- **Pricing:** Free (unlimited transcription, 800 min storage). Pro $10/user/month, Business $19/user/month
- **Bot or bot-free:** Bot-based
- **Best for:** Global teams with multilingual meetings who need affordable, feature-rich transcription

### 4. Otter.ai — Best for in-person and hybrid meetings

[Otter.ai](https://otter.ai/) is the strongest option for in-person and hybrid meetings. The mobile app records in-room conversations with speaker identification — a use case most competitors handle poorly because they are optimized for virtual meetings.

Otter's conversational search lets you ask questions about meeting content ("What did Sarah say about the timeline?") rather than reading through full transcripts.

- **Platforms:** Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, in-person (mobile app)
- **Key features:** In-person recording, conversational search, OtterPilot automation, slide capture, live transcript sharing
- **Pricing:** Free (300 min/month). Pro $16.99/month, Business $30/user/month
- **Bot or bot-free:** Bot-based for virtual, local recording for in-person
- **Best for:** Teams with in-person, hybrid, and virtual meetings who need one tool for all formats

### 5. Granola — Best bot-free private note taker

[Granola](https://www.granola.ai/) is the stealth option. It never announces itself — no bot joins your call, no notification appears to other participants. It captures audio locally from your Mac, processes it after the meeting, and generates structured notes with action items.

This makes Granola the top choice for sensitive meetings, executive conversations, and anyone who finds meeting bots awkward or intrusive. The trade-off: it is Mac-only as of early 2026.

- **Platforms:** Any platform (captures system audio locally) — Zoom, Meet, Teams, phone calls
- **Key features:** Completely invisible to participants, local audio capture, structured notes, action items, Mac-native
- **Pricing:** Free (limited history). Pro $18/month, Business $25/user/month
- **Bot or bot-free:** Completely bot-free (local device recording)
- **Best for:** Executives, consultants, and anyone who needs private meeting notes without other participants knowing

## Bot vs bot-free: which approach to choose

The March 2026 Google Meet update changed the calculation. Google now places third-party bots in a "potential risk" queue, requiring hosts to manually approve them. This makes bot-free tools more practical for Google Meet-heavy teams.

**Choose bot-based** if you want the deepest integrations, highest accuracy, and do not mind the bot appearing in your participant list. Most enterprise features (CRM sync, deal intelligence, coaching) require bot-based recording.

**Choose bot-free** if you have sensitive meetings, client-facing calls where a bot feels unprofessional, or use Google Meet heavily and want to avoid the approval friction. Granola, Jamie, Krisp, tl;dv (desktop app), and Tactiq all offer bot-free options.

## How to choose the right tool

**Solo freelancer or consultant:** Fathom (free unlimited recordings) or Granola (private, bot-free).

**Small team (3-10 people):** tl;dv (custom templates, both bot and bot-free options) or Fireflies (affordable, 100+ languages).

**Sales team:** Avoma (full lifecycle CRM integration) or Gong (conversation intelligence and coaching).

**Enterprise:** Fellow (compliance certifications) or Read.ai (engagement analytics) plus Gong for sales.

**Google Meet only:** Tactiq (live captions, no bot) or Cleft Notes (lightest extension).

**In-person and hybrid:** Otter.ai (best mobile recording) or Notta (104 languages).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best free AI meeting note taker?

Fathom offers the most generous free tier — unlimited recordings with five AI summaries per month and a 5.0/5 G2 rating from over 6,000 reviews. Fireflies offers unlimited transcription with 800 minutes of storage on its free plan. tl;dv provides unlimited recordings and transcripts for free. For bot-free options, Krisp and Granola both offer functional free tiers.

### Do AI meeting bots affect meeting quality?

They can. Some participants feel uncomfortable knowing a bot is recording. Google's March 2026 update places third-party bots in a "potential risk" queue on Google Meet, requiring host approval. Bot-free alternatives like Granola, Jamie, Krisp, and tl;dv's desktop app avoid this issue entirely by recording audio locally from your device.

### Can AI meeting tools replace manual note-taking?

For most meetings, yes. The best tools in 2026 achieve 95 percent transcription accuracy and generate structured summaries with action items within 30 seconds of the call ending. However, for highly technical or specialized discussions, reviewing and editing the AI summary is still recommended.

### Which AI meeting note taker works with Google Meet after the March 2026 bot restrictions?

Bot-free tools work without restrictions: Granola, Jamie, Krisp, tl;dv (desktop app), Tactiq (Chrome extension), and Cleft Notes. Bot-based tools still work but require the meeting host to manually approve the bot from the "potential risk" queue.

### How much do AI meeting note takers cost?

Free tiers are genuinely useful — Fathom, Fireflies, and tl;dv all offer unlimited recordings for free. Paid plans range from $7 per month (Fellow) to $100+ per user per month (Gong). Most teams spend $10 to $30 per user per month for full-featured meeting intelligence. Enterprise conversation intelligence (Gong, Chorus) costs $100 to $150 per user per month.

### Are AI meeting notes secure and private?

Security varies significantly by tool. Fellow holds SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA, GDPR, and GLBA certifications. Bot-free tools like Granola and Jamie process audio locally on your device, which means meeting audio never leaves your machine. Always check the vendor's data processing policies, especially for client-facing or regulated industry meetings.
