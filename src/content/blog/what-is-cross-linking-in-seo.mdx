Cross linking in SEO is connecting related pages on your own website with internal links. It spreads authority between your pages, gives search engines the context to understand how your content relates, helps crawlers discover pages, and shows AI engines the shape of your expertise. Unlike backlinks, every cross link is yours to place, which makes it the most controllable ranking lever you have.

A pile of unconnected pages is weaker than the same pages wired into a network. Cross linking is the wiring.

## What does cross linking actually do?

Four things, all of them useful.

**It distributes authority.** The value a strong page earns can pass through a link to another page. Link from your best pages to the ones you want to rank, and you lift them.

**It builds context.** The pages you connect, and the words you link with, tell search engines and AI systems how your content relates. A cluster of interlinked pages on one topic signals genuine depth.

**It aids discovery.** Crawlers follow internal links to find pages. A page with no internal links pointing to it is hard to discover and slow to index. Cross linking makes sure everything is reachable.

**It keeps people reading.** Good cross links send a reader to the logical next step, which improves engagement and the odds they convert.

Link pages that genuinely relate. Use descriptive anchor text that reflects the destination, so both readers and engines know what they will get. Point links from your high-authority pages toward the pages you most want to rank. And organize content into clusters, a pillar page interlinked with its supporting posts, so your site reads as an expert on the topic rather than a set of stray pages. Keep the important pages within a few clicks of the homepage. The [content strategy chapter](/seo/content-strategy/) covers the cluster model in full.

## Why does cross linking matter more for AI search?

AI engines map the relationships between your pages partly through internal links. A well-linked cluster signals real topical authority on a subject, which makes an engine more confident citing your pages for questions in that area. Disconnected pages signal neither depth nor structure.

Cross linking is free, fully in your control, and compounds as your site grows. Wire your pages together on purpose, and the network ranks better than any single page could alone.

## FAQs

### What is the difference between cross linking and backlinks?

Cross links are internal links between pages on your own site, which you fully control. Backlinks are links from other websites to yours, which you have to earn. Cross linking distributes authority within your site and builds topical context, while backlinks bring authority in from outside.

### Is cross linking good for SEO?

Yes. It distributes authority to the pages you want to rank, gives search engines and AI systems the context to understand how your content relates, helps crawlers discover pages, and keeps readers moving to relevant next steps. Because you control every internal link, it is one of the most reliable on-page levers.

### How do you cross link pages correctly?

Link pages that genuinely relate, use descriptive anchor text instead of "click here," point links from strong pages toward the ones you want to rank, and organize content into clusters with a pillar page linked to its supporting posts. Keep important pages within a few clicks of the homepage.
