## How we check each deal

Every tool was checked on November 17, 2026, on its own pricing page first. A deal counts only if the vendor shows it: a pricing-page banner, a dedicated Black Friday page, a post on the vendor's blog, or its official social account.

When a pricing page blocked our check or showed two different prices, we print "See pricing page" instead of guessing a number.

## What to check before you buy

- **Billing period.** A "50% off" deal on annual billing still means paying a year upfront. Do the math on monthly cost and total cost.
- **Renewal price.** Many deals cover year one only. Put a reminder in your calendar a month before renewal.
- **New customers only.** Adobe's offer is for new customers. Existing subscribers rarely get the headline price.
- **Credits versus seats.** Some AI "deals" discount usage credits, not the plan itself. Read what the percentage applies to.

Short version: buy the tool you would pay full price for anyway, at the lower price. A deal on a tool you won't use is not a saving.
