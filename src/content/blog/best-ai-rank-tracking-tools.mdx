Rank tracking used to be a single, settled question: where does my page sit on the results page for a given keyword? You picked a tool, fed it your keywords, and watched the numbers move. The metric was a number between one and one hundred, and everyone understood what it meant.

That question is still worth answering in 2026, but it is no longer the only one. A growing share of the queries your customers ask never produce a classic ranked list at all. They produce an AI answer, sometimes with citations, sometimes without. When ChatGPT recommends three tools and yours is not one of them, no position tracker in the world will tell you, because there is no position to track.

So rank tracking has split into two jobs. The first job is the old one: monitoring positions in Google and other engines for the keywords that still return ten blue links. The second job is new: monitoring whether you appear inside AI-generated answers across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews. The two layers measure different surfaces, use different methods, and increasingly need different tools. This guide covers both, and explains which kind of team needs which.

- **Best value all-rounder:** [SE Ranking](https://seranking.com), pairing rank tracking with AI tracking in one platform, from around $65/month.
- **Best for unlimited keyword volume:** Nightwatch, with flat-rate tracking at scale, from around $39/month.
- **Best if you already pay for a suite:** Semrush Position Tracking, included with Semrush from $139.95/month.
- **Best for link-led teams:** Ahrefs Rank Tracker, included with Ahrefs from $129/month.
- **Best lightweight tracker for content teams:** Wincher, simple and cheap, from around $29/month.
- **Best budget standalone tracker:** SERPWatcher (Mangools), from around $29/month.
- **Best dedicated AI visibility platform:** [Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com), enterprise AI answer tracking, custom pricing.
- **Best mid-market AI answer tracker:** Otterly.ai, with citation and share-of-voice tracking, from around $29/month.
- **Best AI brand mention tracking inside a suite:** Ahrefs Brand Radar, an add-on to Ahrefs plans.
- **Best for European GEO teams:** Peec AI, for competitive AI visibility benchmarking, from around €90/month.

If you only have budget for one classic tracker and one AI tracker, AccuRanker or SE Ranking on the position side and Otterly.ai or Profound on the AI side cover most real needs.

## Classic rank trackers

These tools answer the original question with precision: where do you rank, for which keyword, in which location, on which device, and how is that changing over time. They remain essential because organic positions still drive the majority of trackable search traffic for most sites, and because AI engines frequently pull from the same top-ranking pages. A page that ranks well is also a page that AI systems are more likely to cite, so classic positions are a leading indicator for AI presence, not a separate concern.

### AccuRanker

AccuRanker is widely regarded as the most accurate dedicated rank tracker, and it is built for speed. Most platforms refresh positions once a day; AccuRanker lets you trigger on-demand refreshes whenever you need a current reading, which matters during a launch or a recovery. It handles large keyword sets without slowing down and integrates cleanly with Google Search Console and Looker Studio.

In 2026 AccuRanker also flags AI Overviews and SERP features at the keyword level, so you can see which of your tracked terms now trigger an AI answer. It is a position tracker first, but it has crossed into the second layer enough to be useful there.

**Pricing:** From around $129/month for 1,000 keywords.

### SE Ranking

SE Ranking is the strongest all-rounder for small and mid-sized teams because it bundles accurate daily rank tracking with a full SEO toolkit (audits, backlink monitoring, keyword research) at a price well below the big suites. Tracking supports granular location targeting down to the city or postcode, plus separate desktop and mobile data.

What lifts SE Ranking above a pure classic tracker is its built-in AI tracking, covered in the next section. For a team that wants positions and AI answer monitoring without paying for two subscriptions, it is the most economical single platform.

**Pricing:** From around $65/month, with AI tracking on higher tiers.

### Nightwatch

Nightwatch is built for scale and location depth. It tracks rankings across more than 100,000 locations and handles very large keyword portfolios on flat-rate plans, which makes it attractive for agencies and sites with sprawling local or international footprints. Its reporting and white-label options are strong.

If your bottleneck is keyword volume rather than feature depth, Nightwatch usually works out cheaper per keyword than the premium trackers.

**Pricing:** From around $39/month.

### Semrush Position Tracking

Semrush Position Tracking is the rank tracking module inside the broader Semrush platform. It is accurate and feature-rich, with competitor comparison, cannibalization detection, and a "Featured Snippets" and AI Overview view that flags which tracked keywords now return an AI answer and whether you are cited within it. For teams already paying for Semrush, there is little reason to buy a separate tracker.

The trade-off is cost and update cadence: position data refreshes daily on most plans, which is fine for ongoing monitoring but slower than AccuRanker for rapid checks.

**Pricing:** Included with Semrush, from $139.95/month.

### Ahrefs Rank Tracker

Ahrefs Rank Tracker comes with every Ahrefs subscription and shines for teams whose work is link-led. It reports positions alongside the visibility, traffic, and SERP feature data that Ahrefs is known for, and it flags AI Overviews at the keyword level. The "Competitors" overview is one of the clearer share-of-visibility views in the category.

Like Semrush, it makes most sense as part of the suite rather than as a standalone purchase, since the rank tracker alone does not justify the Ahrefs price.

**Pricing:** Included with Ahrefs, from $129/month.

### Wincher

Wincher is the lightweight option content teams reach for when the big suites feel like overkill. It does one thing, tracks keyword positions, and does it cleanly, with daily updates, a usable WordPress plugin, and straightforward reporting. There is a free tier for a handful of keywords.

It will not replace Semrush or Ahrefs for competitive analysis, but for a writer or a small site that just needs to know whether positions are moving, it is fast and inexpensive.

**Pricing:** From around $29/month, with a limited free tier.

### SERPWatcher

SERPWatcher is the rank tracker inside the Mangools toolset, and it is among the friendliest options for solo operators and small businesses. Its signature "Performance Index" rolls your tracked keywords into a single trend number so you can see overall movement at a glance without reading a table. Setup is quick and the interface is uncluttered.

It is best understood as a budget standalone tracker. Depth is limited compared with the premium platforms, but for the price the accuracy is solid.

**Pricing:** From around $29/month as part of Mangools.

## AI visibility and answer trackers

This is the layer that did not exist a few years ago. These tools do not check where you rank in Google. They check whether you appear inside AI-generated answers: are you cited when someone asks ChatGPT for a recommendation, do you show up in Perplexity's sources, does the Google AI Overview mention your brand, and how does your presence compare with competitors across all of those surfaces.

The core metrics shift accordingly. Instead of average position, you track citation frequency (how often an AI answer references you), share of voice (your slice of AI mentions versus competitors for a topic), and sentiment (whether the mention is favorable). If those metrics are new to you, our guide on [how to measure AI visibility](/blog/how-to-measure-ai-visibility/) and the breakdown of [AEO KPIs and metrics](/blog/aeo-kpis-metrics-ai-visibility/) explain what to watch and why. For the underlying concept, see [what AI visibility is](/blog/what-is-ai-visibility/).

### Profound

Profound is the most established enterprise-grade AI visibility platform. It tracks how your brand appears across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and other answer engines, monitoring citations, share of voice, and the prompts that surface you (or your competitors). Its "Answer Engine Insights" pull apart which sources AI models cite for your category, which tells you where to earn mentions, not just whether you have them.

Profound is built for brands that treat AI visibility as a serious channel, with the data depth and reporting to match. Pricing is custom and lands at the enterprise end, so it suits in-house teams and agencies rather than solo sites.

**Best for:** Enterprises and agencies that need deep, multi-platform AI citation analytics.

### Otterly.ai

Otterly.ai brought AI answer tracking down to a price mid-market teams can afford. It monitors your brand and chosen prompts across ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, and Perplexity, reporting how often you are mentioned, whether you are linked, and where you sit relative to competitors. You define the prompts you care about and it tracks your presence in the answers over time.

It is the most approachable on-ramp into the AI visibility layer: enough depth to be genuinely useful, priced for teams that are testing the channel rather than betting the budget on it.

**Pricing:** From around $29/month.

### Ahrefs Brand Radar

Ahrefs Brand Radar tracks brand mentions across AI Overviews and AI answer surfaces, and because it lives inside Ahrefs, it ties that data to the link and content data you already track there. It reports how often your brand appears in AI answers for relevant prompts and how that compares with competitors, which makes it a natural fit for teams that want one vendor for both layers rather than a separate AI subscription.

It is more a brand-mention monitor than a full prompt-level GEO platform, but for Ahrefs customers it adds real AI visibility coverage without a new tool.

**Pricing:** Add-on to Ahrefs subscriptions.

### SE Ranking AI tracking

SE Ranking's AI tracking sits alongside its classic rank tracker, which is what makes the platform unusual. The same project can show your Google positions and your presence in AI Overviews and chatbot answers, so you see both layers side by side without exporting between tools. It reports whether AI answers mention or link your site for the prompts you track, and how that changes over time.

For a small or mid-sized team, this single-platform approach is the cleanest way to cover positions and AI presence at once. It will not match a dedicated platform like Profound for AI-specific depth, but it covers the essentials at a fraction of the combined cost.

**Pricing:** Included on higher SE Ranking tiers.

### Peec AI

Peec AI is a European-built AI visibility platform focused on competitive benchmarking. It tracks how often your brand appears across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews for your category prompts, and frames the results as a head-to-head against named competitors, so you see your share of AI voice rather than just your raw mention count. Teams use it to find the prompts where rivals are winning citations and you are absent.

It has gained traction with GEO-focused agencies and brands that want competitor-relative reporting rather than a single-brand dashboard.

**Pricing:** From around €90/month.

## How to choose

You do not need every tool, and you may not need both layers yet. Work through three questions.

**Do you need AI tracking at all right now?** Check whether your important queries actually return AI answers and whether your category shows up in ChatGPT or Perplexity recommendations. Ask the chatbots a handful of buyer questions in your space and see who gets named. If AI answers rarely trigger for your keywords, or your category is barely represented, a classic tracker is enough for now and you can revisit the AI layer in a quarter or two. If AI Overviews fire on your money terms and competitors are being recommended by name, you are already losing visibility you cannot see, and the AI layer is no longer optional.

**What is your budget?** A solo operator or small site can run a single platform: SE Ranking gives you both layers for roughly $65 to $120/month, which is far cheaper than buying a premium classic tracker and a dedicated AI platform separately. Mid-market teams that want best-in-class on each side often pair AccuRanker for positions with Otterly.ai for AI presence. Enterprises with AI visibility as a named channel justify Profound or Peec AI on top of whatever suite they already run.

**What is your scale?** Keyword volume and location depth push you toward Nightwatch or AccuRanker on the classic side. Many domains, many markets, and the need for white-label reporting favor agency-friendly platforms. A handful of keywords and a content workflow point to Wincher or SERPWatcher. Match the tool to the size of the job, not to the longest feature list.

A reasonable 2026 default for most growing teams: one accurate classic tracker, plus one AI visibility tool, reviewed together each month so positions and citations sit on the same dashboard. Definitions for any of the terms above live in the [glossary](/glossary/).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best rank tracking tool overall in 2026?

For pure classic position tracking, AccuRanker is the strongest choice because of its accuracy and on-demand refreshes. For teams that want both classic positions and AI answer tracking in one affordable platform, SE Ranking is the best overall value. The right answer depends on whether you need the AI layer yet: if you do, a single platform that covers both, or a classic tracker paired with a dedicated AI tool, beats any single classic tracker on its own.

### What is the best budget rank tracker?

Wincher and SERPWatcher both start around $29/month and handle classic position tracking well for small sites and content teams. Nightwatch is the best budget option if your constraint is keyword volume rather than features, thanks to its flat-rate plans. For a budget tool that also touches the AI layer, SE Ranking's lower tiers are hard to beat.

### Do keyword rankings still matter in AI search?

Yes. Classic positions still drive the majority of trackable organic traffic for most sites, and they are also a leading indicator of AI presence, because AI engines frequently cite the same pages that rank well in traditional results. Rankings have not stopped mattering; they have stopped being the whole picture. You now track positions and AI answer presence as two layers of the same visibility question.

### What is the best tool for tracking AI citations?

For enterprise depth across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews, Profound is the leading dedicated platform. For mid-market teams, Otterly.ai offers citation and share-of-voice tracking at a far lower price. Peec AI is strong for competitor-relative benchmarking, especially for European and GEO-focused teams, and Ahrefs Brand Radar adds AI mention tracking for existing Ahrefs customers.

### Can one tool track both classic rankings and AI citations?

Yes, and that is increasingly the point. SE Ranking is the clearest example, pairing accurate daily rank tracking with AI Overview and chatbot answer monitoring in a single project. Semrush and Ahrefs also flag AI Overviews within their position trackers, though their dedicated AI mention tracking is lighter than a purpose-built platform like Profound. For most growing teams, one combined platform is the simplest starting point, with a specialist AI tool added later if the channel grows.

### How often should I check AI visibility versus rankings?

Classic positions are worth a daily or weekly glance because they move quickly and respond to on-page and link changes. AI visibility moves more slowly and is noisier, so a monthly review of citation frequency, share of voice, and sentiment is usually enough. Reviewing both on the same monthly dashboard keeps the two layers connected, since a gain in rankings often precedes a gain in AI citations for the same query.
