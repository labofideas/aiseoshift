Every major SEO platform responded to AI search by shipping new features in 2025 and 2026. Semrush launched its AI Toolkit. Ahrefs released Brand Radar. Moz updated its Domain Authority model and added AI intent signals. Surfer doubled down on content scoring for AI-generated answers. The press releases all sound similar.

The results are not.

Some of these platforms added genuine AI visibility infrastructure, tools that track citations, diagnose why content is not being cited, and surface actionable fixes. Others added AI-flavored interfaces over the same traditional SEO data that existed five years ago. Knowing which is which matters a great deal if AI search visibility is a business priority.

This comparison evaluates all four platforms across four criteria that actually predict AI citation performance: AI citation tracking, schema generation tools, content optimization for answer engines, and keyword research for AI query types. We are not comparing their traditional SEO capabilities, all four are excellent for that. We are comparing what they add for the new discipline of [getting cited by AI search systems](/blog/how-to-get-cited-by-ai-search-systems/).

## Quick answer: which platform wins for AI SEO

**Semrush** is the best choice for teams that want a single platform covering both traditional SEO and AI visibility monitoring. Its AI Toolkit, covering ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Perplexity, and Gemini, is the most complete integrated solution in the category.

**Ahrefs** is the better choice if your priority is question-format keyword research and competitive backlink intelligence, with Brand Radar as a solid secondary AI visibility layer.

**Moz** trails on dedicated AI visibility features but remains competitive for technical SEO auditing and authority metrics.

**Surfer SEO** has the most effective content optimization workflow for pages targeting AI-generated answer positions, but it is a content tool, not a platform, it does not replace a primary SEO suite.

The honest summary: for pure AI visibility work, Semrush wins. For integrated SEO with AI visibility as a supplementary layer, Ahrefs is the stronger alternative. Moz and Surfer both serve specific use cases without offering comprehensive AI SEO coverage.

## Evaluation criteria

The four criteria below were chosen because they map directly to the variables that determine whether a page gets cited in AI-generated answers. For the metric framework that underlies this evaluation, see [AEO KPIs and metrics for AI visibility](/blog/aeo-kpis-metrics-ai-visibility/).

**AI citation tracking.** Can the platform tell you whether your brand is being mentioned in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, or Gemini responses? Can it benchmark your citation rate against competitors?

**Schema tools.** Does the platform help you implement and audit [schema markup for AI visibility](/blog/schema-markup-for-ai-visibility/), the structured data signals that make content machine-readable for AI systems?

**Content optimization for answer engines.** Does the platform's content guidance address question-answer structure, entity coverage, and citation-friendliness, not just keyword density?

**Keyword research for AI queries.** Can the platform surface question-format keywords, AI Overview triggers, and conversational query clusters rather than just volume-ranked keyword lists?

## Semrush: the most complete AI SEO platform

[Semrush](https://www.semrush.com) built the most comprehensive AI SEO feature set of any traditional platform. The AI Toolkit, launched in 2025 and substantially expanded since, covers five AI platforms: ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Perplexity, and Gemini. It provides Share of Voice tracking, sentiment monitoring, and competitive benchmarking against up to nine rivals, all within the same platform teams already use for keyword research, site auditing, and link building.

The most distinctive element is the prompt database: over 239 million real user prompts that reveal exactly which questions trigger AI-generated responses in any given topic area. This is not synthetic data, it is sourced from actual AI search usage, which means the keyword intelligence reflects how people actually query AI systems rather than how they once queried Google. For teams trying to [identify AI keyword opportunities](/blog/best-ai-keyword-research-tools/), this database is a structural advantage that competitors have not matched.

The AI Search Site Audit function connects visibility gaps directly to technical and content fixes. Instead of presenting a metric and leaving teams to diagnose root causes themselves, it surfaces specific recommendations: pages missing Article schema, FAQ schema opportunities, content with low entity density, and pages that have been crawled but not cited. That closes the loop between measurement and action in a way that separate tools cannot.

**Schema capabilities.** Semrush's Site Audit catches schema errors, missing markup, and invalid JSON-LD. The AI Search Site Audit adds AI-specific schema recommendations, identifying pages where FAQPage schema would increase citation probability, or where Article schema metadata is incomplete. It does not generate schema code automatically, but it identifies what is needed with enough precision that implementation is straightforward.

**Where Semrush falls short.** The AI Toolkit is an add-on for existing subscribers, not a standalone product. Teams not already using Semrush face paying for the full platform to access it. Schema generation still requires external tools or manual implementation. And because Semrush is so broad, teams focused exclusively on AI visibility may be paying for more traditional SEO functionality than they need.

**Pricing.** Semrush Pro starts at $139.95/month. Semrush One (Classic + AI Toolkit) is $199/month.

**Best for:** Teams already using Semrush for traditional SEO who want to extend their workflow into AI visibility monitoring without switching platforms.

## Ahrefs: strong keyword research, solid AI visibility layer

[Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com) is the strongest platform for question-format keyword research, a foundational task for AI SEO, and it has added meaningful AI visibility capabilities through Brand Radar and a 250 million real-user prompt database.

The Keywords Explorer's Questions mode is the clearest tool in any platform for surfacing question-format queries at scale. Enter a seed keyword and filter by question type, what, how, why, which, can, does, and you get a ranked list of questions with volume estimates, difficulty scores, and SERP feature data showing whether each query already triggers a featured snippet, People Also Ask box, or AI Overview. That SERP feature column is genuinely useful: when a keyword shows both a PAA box and an AI Overview, it is a confirmed AI trigger, and knowing which of your target keywords have confirmed triggers helps prioritize content investment.

Brand Radar tracks AI citations across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, Google AI Overviews, and Google AI Mode, a broader platform list than Semrush, which is notable. It includes competitive benchmarking against named rivals and shows which AI platforms cite your brand most and least frequently. Ahrefs also separately attributes AI traffic in its analytics, breaking out ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini as distinct referral sources. Most analytics setups lump this traffic into "referrals", that attribution clarity is practically valuable.

**Schema capabilities.** Ahrefs Site Audit detects structured data errors, invalid JSON-LD, missing required properties, schema validation failures, but it does not generate schema or provide AI-specific schema recommendations beyond error detection. It is a good catch-all auditing layer but not a deep schema optimization tool.

**Where Ahrefs falls short.** Brand Radar's full AI index is a $699/month add-on, which is a significant jump from the base subscription. The platform also has a content optimization gap, Ahrefs does not have a native content editor or NLP scoring tool comparable to Semrush's Writing Assistant or Surfer's Content Editor. Teams using Ahrefs for AI SEO will need a separate content optimization tool. For a deeper look at how to read [AI analytics data](/blog/best-ai-analytics-tools-seo/), the signal isolation methods that work in Ahrefs' traffic attribution are covered in detail.

**Pricing.** Ahrefs Lite starts at $129/month. Brand Radar with full AI indexes is a $699/month add-on.

**Best for:** Teams that prioritize question-format keyword research and competitive backlink intelligence, with Brand Radar as a secondary AI visibility layer.

## Moz: solid traditional SEO, limited AI-specific features

Moz remains a strong platform for authority measurement, technical site auditing, and local SEO, areas where its Domain Authority metric and MozBar browser extension still see wide use. Its AI SEO capabilities, however, lag behind Semrush and Ahrefs by a meaningful margin.

Moz's keyword research tools apply intent classification, informational, navigational, commercial, transactional, which provides a useful proxy for identifying content that AI systems prefer to cite. Informational-intent queries are the ones most likely to trigger AI Overviews, so the intent filter does serve a relevant function. But Moz lacks any dedicated AI citation tracking, branded AI monitoring, or prompt-level keyword intelligence. The platform was built on a link-graph and authority model, and its 2026 updates have not fundamentally changed that orientation.

Page Optimization in Moz Pro provides content scoring against a target keyword, with recommendations for content length, keyword usage, and page structure. It is a basic version of what Surfer does more precisely, and it does not account for the question-answer structure that AI citation engines favor.

**Schema capabilities.** Moz's Site Crawl identifies structured data errors at the crawl level, similar to Ahrefs. It does not offer schema generation or AI-specific schema recommendations.

**Where Moz stands.** For teams that already have Moz as their primary SEO platform and want to add AI-specific monitoring, the honest recommendation is to pair Moz with a dedicated AEO tool rather than waiting for Moz to ship AI visibility features. Moz's core strengths, its link index, local SEO tools, and SERP analysis, complement dedicated AI visibility tools well. For the technical SEO foundation that makes AI citation possible, including an [AI SEO audit checklist](/blog/ai-seo-audit-checklist/) perspective, Moz's crawl data is useful input even if it is not a complete AI SEO solution on its own.

**Pricing.** Moz Pro starts at $99/month.

**Best for:** Teams that need strong local SEO, link authority measurement, and technical site auditing, with AI SEO supplemented by a dedicated AEO tool.

## Surfer SEO: the best content optimization workflow

Surfer SEO takes a different approach than the three platforms above. It is a content optimization tool, not a full SEO suite, it does not track backlinks, monitor AI citations, or run technical site audits. What it does, it does better than any other tool: NLP-based content scoring that tells you exactly what a page needs to cover, how to structure it, and what entity gaps exist relative to the top-ranking pages for any given query.

Surfer's Content Editor analyzes the top pages competing for a target keyword and extracts their semantic content patterns, terms used, question coverage, heading structure, content length, and NLP entity density. It then produces a real-time content score as you write or edit, with specific recommendations for what to add or remove. The scoring model has been updated to reflect AI ranking factors, with emphasis on question coverage and entity completeness, the two content signals that most strongly predict AI Overview inclusion.

For the content layer of AI SEO, the actual page-level optimization that determines whether well-structured content earns citations, Surfer is the most useful tool in this comparison. An experienced team using Surfer alongside a platform like Semrush or Ahrefs covers both the visibility monitoring and the content optimization layers effectively. Teams trying to rely on Surfer alone will find significant gaps in keyword research, citation tracking, and technical SEO.

Surfer's Audit feature also provides content scoring for existing pages against their current ranking queries, which is useful for identifying which pages are closest to the citation threshold and need incremental optimization rather than full rewrites.

**Schema capabilities.** Surfer does not have dedicated schema tools. Some heading-level recommendations implicitly encourage FAQ-format content, which benefits from FAQPage schema, but the tool does not generate or audit structured data.

**Pricing.** Surfer SEO starts at $99/month (Essential). The Scale plan at $219/month adds team features and higher content editor capacity.

**Best for:** Content teams optimizing pages for AI Overview and answer engine positions. Best used alongside Semrush or Ahrefs, not as a standalone platform.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which SEO platform has the best AI citation tracking in 2026?**

Semrush has the most integrated AI citation tracking of any traditional SEO platform, covering ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Perplexity, and Gemini within a single dashboard. Ahrefs Brand Radar covers six platforms but requires a $699/month add-on for the full AI index. For teams that prioritize citation tracking as the primary use case, dedicated AEO platforms like Profound or Otterly.AI typically provide more depth than either traditional platform.

**Does Ahrefs or Semrush do better for question-format keyword research?**

Both are strong, but with different strengths. Semrush's 239 million real-user prompt database gives it an edge for AI Overview trigger identification, it can surface which questions actually trigger AI-generated responses. Ahrefs Questions mode is cleaner for systematic question mining by topic and gives better SERP feature data for individual queries. Teams serious about AI keyword research often use both tools in combination.

**Is Surfer SEO worth adding to an existing SEO stack?**

Yes, if content quality is a bottleneck. Surfer's NLP-based content scoring is more precise than the content optimization guidance in Semrush, Ahrefs, or Moz. For teams publishing content targeting AI Overview and answer engine positions, the Content Editor's real-time scoring saves revision cycles and directly improves the entity coverage and question-answer structure that AI citation systems favor.

**Can Moz Pro be used for AI SEO in 2026?**

Moz Pro is useful for the technical and authority foundations of AI SEO, site auditing, link intelligence, and authority measurement, but it lacks dedicated AI citation tracking, prompt-level keyword intelligence, and AI-specific content optimization guidance. Teams using Moz as their primary platform should pair it with a dedicated AEO tool for the monitoring layer rather than expecting Moz's existing features to cover AI visibility.

**Do any of these platforms generate schema markup automatically?**

None of the four platforms in this comparison generate schema markup automatically. All four (except Surfer) can audit existing schema and detect errors. For schema generation, teams typically use dedicated schema generators, CMS plugins with schema support, or manual JSON-LD implementation guided by their SEO platform's audit recommendations. Given how central schema is to AI citation performance, this is a genuine gap across the category.

**How much does it cost to run a complete AI SEO stack using these tools?**

A functional AI SEO stack using these platforms runs between $270 and $950 per month depending on which combination you choose. A common setup, Semrush One at $199/month plus Surfer Scale at $219/month, covers keyword research, AI citation tracking, technical auditing, and content optimization for around $420/month. Teams needing Ahrefs Brand Radar's full AI index face a higher ceiling. The economics improve as team size grows, since most platforms include multiple users at the stated price.
