Ten SEO content writing tools worth a look, picked for doing one job well rather than trying to be everything: NeuronWriter, ContentShake AI, Byword, Junia AI, GravityWrite, Copysmith, SE Ranking, Rankability, Outranking, and BrandWell. This list deliberately leaves out ChatGPT, Claude, Jasper, Copy.ai, Writesonic, Frase, Surfer, Scalenut, Clearscope, and MarketMuse, not because they are bad, but because we already cover them elsewhere and everyone recommends them anyway. These ten are the ones that came up while actually doing the work, each solving a specific, narrower problem than "write me an article."

If you want the household names ranked head to head, [best AI writing tools for SEO](/blog/best-ai-writing-tools-for-seo/) and [best Surfer SEO alternatives](/blog/best-surfer-seo-alternatives/) cover those directly. This list starts where those leave off.

## The ten, and what each one is actually for

### NeuronWriter: semantic scoring on a budget

NeuronWriter builds content briefs and scores your draft against the semantic terms top-ranking pages actually use, the same category of job Surfer does, at a noticeably lower price. Plans start at $23 a month for the Bronze tier, moving up to $45, $69, and $93 as you add content volume and features.

Best for: a solo writer or small team that wants semantic scoring without Surfer's price tag.

### ContentShake AI: writing inside the Semrush ecosystem

ContentShake AI is Semrush's own content-writing tool, available standalone at $60 a month with unlimited AI-generated articles, or included free inside Semrush's Pro plan and above. If you already pay for Semrush, this is writing help you are likely leaving unused rather than a new subscription.

Best for: existing Semrush customers who want writing assistance without adding another tool to the stack.

### Byword: writes and publishes directly to your CMS

Byword researches the keyword, drafts in your brand voice using a mix of models under the hood, GPT-5.4, Claude Opus 4.6, and Gemini 3.1 Pro among them, and then publishes straight to WordPress, Webflow, and several other CMSs without you copying and pasting a draft anywhere. The publish-directly step is the actual differentiator; most writing tools stop at a document.

Best for: teams that want the draft-to-published pipeline handled end to end, not just the draft.

### Junia AI: multilingual SEO articles

Junia AI is built for producing SEO-oriented articles with real multilingual support, useful the moment your content strategy needs more than English. It functions broadly, closer to a general digital-writing toolkit than a narrow specialist, which suits a team that wants one subscription covering several content formats.

Best for: sites publishing in more than one language who do not want a separate tool per language.

### GravityWrite: broad, budget content generation

GravityWrite covers a wide range of content types, blog posts, social captions, product copy, at a price point aimed at solo creators and small budgets rather than agencies. It will not out-optimize a dedicated semantic tool, but for the price it covers a surprising amount of ground.

Best for: a tight budget that needs to cover multiple content formats, not just long-form articles.

### Copysmith: ecommerce and marketing copy at scale

Copysmith leans toward marketing copy specifically, product descriptions, ad variations, landing pages, rather than long-form editorial writing, which makes it a better fit for an ecommerce team managing hundreds of product listings than for a blog-focused content strategy.

Best for: ecommerce teams and marketers who need many short, repetitive copy pieces rather than long articles.

### SE Ranking: content writing bundled into a full SEO suite

SE Ranking's content tool is one part of a larger platform that also covers rank tracking, technical audits, and backlink analysis, with the Core plan starting around $103 a month billed annually. The appeal is not the writing tool in isolation, it is not chasing Surfer or NeuronWriter on pure optimization depth, but having writing live inside the same login as the rest of your SEO work.

Best for: a team that wants one platform for writing and the rest of their SEO tracking rather than stitching several tools together.

### Rankability: the agency-built trio

Rankability packages three connected tools, Advisor for SERP analysis and recommendations, Researcher for keyword and topic research, and Copywriter for AI drafting based on Advisor's brief, aimed specifically at small to mid-size agencies that need predictable output across many client sites. It starts at $199 a month, a real investment that assumes agency-scale usage to justify it.

Best for: agencies running content production across multiple clients who want one connected workflow rather than separate keyword, brief, and writing tools.

### Outranking: SERP-driven briefs with a guided editor

Outranking builds its brief from actual SERP data, keyword suggestions, and an actionable checklist, then walks you through drafting inside a guided editor rather than handing you a document and a score. Its SEO Writer plan runs $79 a month for 15 documents and two seats.

Best for: a content team that wants the brief and the writing guidance built from the same SERP data, not two separate tools that do not talk to each other.

### BrandWell (formerly Content at Scale): bulk long-form output

BrandWell, the rebrand of Content at Scale, is built for volume: it can produce a detailed long-form article from a single keyword in minutes, pulling entities and structure from what is already ranking. Pricing has widened with the rebrand, a Starter tier around $49 a month up through Agency plans near $499 a month, plus a pay-per-post custom option starting around $4 a post for teams that want to scale without a flat subscription.

Best for: a team or agency that needs genuine volume, many articles across many topics, and is willing to edit AI output rather than write from scratch every time.

## How to actually choose between these

Match the tool to the specific gap, not to the longest feature list. If your problem is that you already have a Semrush login and keep opening a separate writing tool, ContentShake AI closes that gap for free. If your problem is that drafts sit finished and unpublished for days because someone has to manually move them into WordPress, Byword's direct-publish is worth more than a slightly better content score. If your problem is genuine volume, more topics than your team can write by hand, BrandWell is built for exactly that, with the understanding that every output still needs a human edit pass before it goes live, covered in [how content quality affects SEO](/blog/how-content-quality-affects-seo/).

None of these ten replace the fundamentals. A tool can help you write faster and cover the right terms; it cannot manufacture real expertise or a genuine point of view, which is still what separates content that ranks from content that only looks optimized. The full case for that distinction is in [how content quality affects SEO](/blog/how-content-quality-affects-seo/), and the broader roundup of the better-known platforms these ten sit alongside is in [best AI writing tools in 2026](/blog/best-ai-writing-tools/).

## The bottom line

NeuronWriter for semantic scoring without Surfer's price. ContentShake AI if you already pay for Semrush. Byword if publishing, not just drafting, is the bottleneck. Junia AI for multilingual content. GravityWrite on a tight budget. Copysmith for ecommerce copy at scale. SE Ranking if you want writing inside your existing SEO suite. Rankability for agency-scale, multi-client workflows. Outranking for SERP-driven briefs with guided drafting. BrandWell when the job is genuine volume.

Pick the one that closes your actual gap. The household names are still there if none of these ten fit.

## FAQs

### What is the cheapest SEO content writing tool on this list?

NeuronWriter starts the lowest, at $23 a month for its Bronze plan, and still includes real semantic content scoring against top-ranking competitors. GravityWrite and BrandWell's Starter tier, around $49 a month, are the next cheapest, though BrandWell's per-post custom pricing from roughly $4 a post can work out cheaper still for genuinely low volume.

### Are these tools better than ChatGPT or Claude for SEO content?

Not necessarily better, different. ChatGPT and Claude are general-purpose writers you direct yourself. These ten are purpose-built for a specific step in the SEO content workflow, semantic scoring, SERP-driven briefs, direct CMS publishing, bulk production, which a general assistant does not do natively. Many teams use a general assistant alongside one of these for the specialized step.

### Can I use more than one of these tools together?

Yes, and it is common. A typical stack might use one tool for the brief and semantic scoring and a separate one for bulk drafting or direct publishing, since few of these ten try to do every step themselves. Match each tool to the one job it does best rather than expecting a single subscription to replace your whole workflow.
