A new chiropractic patient researching their first visit will check the clinic's Google Business Profile before they make a phone call. The decision to book is often made inside that GBP panel — the reviews, the photos, the hours, the response patterns. Clinics with strong profiles convert searches into appointments. Clinics with weak profiles get the searcher's traffic but lose them to a better-presented competitor.

This is the tactical playbook. Eight sections, every one of them implementable this week.

## Section 1: Categories

Primary category: Chiropractor.

Secondary categories — add every one that genuinely fits:

- Pain Control Clinic
- Sports Medicine Clinic
- Massage Therapist (if you offer it as a service)
- Physical Therapist (if licensed PTs work at the clinic)
- Wellness Center

Do not add categories you do not actually provide. Mismatch between categories and the work shown in reviews and photos gets the profile demoted.

## Section 2: Business description

You get 750 characters. Most clinics use 200. Use them all.

Strong descriptions include:

- Years in practice
- Doctor credentials and continuing education
- Specialties (sports injuries, auto accidents, pregnancy, pediatric)
- Treatment techniques (Activator, diversified, Gonstead, etc.)
- Insurance acceptance
- Same-day appointment availability
- Any unique modalities or equipment

Avoid generic phrases like "providing quality chiropractic care." AI engines extract distinctive content. Boilerplate gets ignored.

## Section 3: Service area

Cities and zip codes you actually serve. Be specific, not aspirational. Five accurate service areas outrank fifteen claimed ones.

## Section 4: Services list

The services section is criminally underused. List every service with descriptions:

- Chiropractic adjustment / spinal manipulation
- Sciatica treatment
- Auto accident injury care
- Sports injury treatment
- Pregnancy chiropractic
- Headache treatment
- Spinal decompression
- Active release technique
- Soft tissue therapy
- Custom orthotics (if offered)

Each service gets 50 to 80 words. AI engines extract these directly when matching patient queries to clinics.

## Section 5: Hours including same-day availability

Hours field. Get this right. Include:

- Standard weekly hours
- Same-day appointment availability (if offered)
- Saturday or evening hours specifically called out
- Holiday hours updated proactively

Inaccurate hours produce poor reviews and damage local pack rankings.

## Section 6: Photos

The photo set that converts:

- Clinic exterior with signage
- Reception and waiting area
- Treatment rooms
- Equipment (especially specialized equipment)
- Doctor and team photos (real, not stock)
- Branded merchandise or community involvement

Upload 30 to 60 photos initially, add 5 to 10 per month. Stale photo profiles lose local pack share.

## Section 7: Posts — weekly minimum

Three post types that work for chiropractic clinics:

1. **Condition spotlight posts** linking to your condition pages
2. **Patient education posts** (stretches, ergonomic tips, healthy habits)
3. **New patient promotions and offers**

Cadence: one to two posts per week.

## Section 8: Q&A — seed common questions

Pre-empt common patient questions:

- Do you take my insurance?
- Do you offer same-day appointments?
- How long is the first visit?
- Do you treat children?
- Do you accept walk-ins?
- Do you offer payment plans?
- Are your techniques safe during pregnancy?

Seeded Q&A populates the GBP panel and feeds AI Overviews.

## What to do this week

A 7-day GBP rebuild:

- **Day 1:** Audit categories and business description. Update.
- **Day 2:** Add 20 new photos. Tag descriptively.
- **Day 3:** Build out services list with descriptions.
- **Day 4:** Seed 10 to 12 Q&As.
- **Day 5:** Schedule first month of posts.
- **Day 6:** Verify hours and service area accuracy.
- **Day 7:** Respond to last 30 reviews. Set up notification system.

## FAQs

### Can solo doctors have a separate GBP from the clinic?

If you operate under a single business name, one GBP. If multiple doctors operate independently under different business names at the same address, separate GBPs are acceptable.

### What about multi-location clinics?

One GBP per physical location, each with its own address, phone, and reviews. Trying to run one GBP for multiple locations gets suspended.

### How do I handle HIPAA compliance in reviews and Q&A?

Never confirm or deny that someone is a patient. Never share specific treatment details. Standard response templates handle this without HIPAA issues. See the [chiropractor reviews post](/blog/chiropractor-reviews-and-ai-recommendations/) for templates.

### Can I run a virtual chiropractic GBP?

No. GBP requires a physical address where you provide care. Telehealth-only chiropractic does not qualify for traditional GBP.
