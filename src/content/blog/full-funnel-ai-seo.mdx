Most AI SEO strategy focuses on getting cited somewhere -- in any AI answer for any relevant query. That is better than nothing, but it is not a strategy. A real strategy maps AI search visibility to the buying journey: different AI engines, different query types, and different content requirements at each stage.

Here is how the full funnel works in the AI search era.

## Why the funnel matters for AI SEO

AI engines do not behave uniformly across the buyer journey. A user in early awareness mode asks broad educational questions. A buyer in consideration mode asks comparison and evaluation questions. A buyer at decision stage asks local, specific, and vendor-validation questions.

Each of these query types surfaces in different AI engines, triggers different AI citation criteria, and requires different content types and structures to win the citation.

Optimizing only for one stage -- typically awareness, because it has the highest query volume -- leaves the consideration and decision stages where competitor citations convert buyers, not yours.

## Top of funnel: Awareness stage

**Query pattern:** Broad informational -- "what is [concept]," "how does [process] work," "why do [businesses/people] need [service]."

**AI engines most active here:** Google AI Overviews (48% of all queries), Perplexity, ChatGPT for educational queries.

**Citation criteria at this stage:** Topical authority and comprehensiveness. AI engines cite sources that cover a topic thoroughly and accurately. Generic overviews are rarely cited; expert explanations with genuine depth are.

**Content types that earn TOFU citations:**
- Comprehensive topic explainers covering a concept end-to-end
- Industry glossaries with authoritative definitions
- "What is" and "How does" guides with expert authorship attribution
- Statistical or research-based content with original data

**The TOFU citation goal:** Appear as the cited source when someone in your target market first encounters your industry or category through an AI engine. This builds early brand exposure at zero acquisition cost -- the user sees your brand as the authority before they are even actively buying.

**Key tactic:** Implement Speakable schema on the summary sections of educational content. Google explicitly recommends this for content intended for AI-generated answer inclusion.

## Middle of funnel: Consideration stage

**Query pattern:** Comparison and evaluation -- "best [solution type] for [use case]," "[option A] vs [option B]," "how to choose [service/product]," "what should I look for in [vendor/solution]."

**AI engines most active here:** Perplexity (comparison queries are a core use case), ChatGPT (used for structured evaluation research), Google AI Mode (used for complex research with no blue links).

**Citation criteria at this stage:** Specificity and usefulness to the decision. AI engines cite sources that help the user evaluate their options -- not content that recommends the source's own products without context.

**Content types that earn MOFU citations:**
- Objective comparison frameworks ("how to evaluate [category]" with specific criteria)
- Buyer's guides with genuine evaluation criteria, not just feature lists
- Case studies with specific outcomes and conditions under which they applied
- FAQ content addressing the specific questions buyers ask when evaluating options

**The MOFU citation goal:** Appear as a trusted source in the AI answers that buyers are reading when they are actively evaluating options in your category. This is the highest-intent citation stage -- users are ready to buy, they are just choosing who.

**Key tactic:** Create content that evaluates your category objectively, not just your product or service. "How to choose a [logistics provider]" content is more AI-citable and more trusted by buyers than "why choose [our company]" content. You can include your own positioning in the latter portion of objective evaluation content.

## Bottom of funnel: Decision stage

**Query pattern:** Local, specific, and validation -- "best [service] near [location]," "[brand name] reviews," "is [company] reliable for [specific use case]," "[service] price in [city]."

**AI engines most active here:** Google AI Mode and AI Overviews (for local decision queries), ChatGPT (local search via Bing), Microsoft Copilot (B2B decision stage research in enterprise tools).

**Citation criteria at this stage:** Trust signals and local/specific relevance. Review volume, credential verification, entity completeness, and local data accuracy.

**Content types that earn BOFU citations:**
- Location-specific landing pages with LocalBusiness schema
- Review aggregation and response management
- Credential and certification pages with verifiable information
- Transparent pricing or cost range pages
- FAQ content addressing common decision-stage objections

**The BOFU citation goal:** Appear in the AI answer when a buyer is specifically looking for a vendor, provider, or local service in your category. This is the citation that directly drives appointments, quote requests, and purchases.

**Key tactic:** Implement complete LocalBusiness schema (for local businesses) or Organization schema with `areaServed` and `serviceType` (for non-local service businesses). These are the entity signals AI engines check when validating whether to cite a source for decision-stage queries.

## Cross-funnel measurement

Each funnel stage requires different metrics:

| Stage | Primary metrics |
|-------|----------------|
| TOFU | Brand citation rate, AI mention share for educational queries |
| MOFU | Citation frequency in comparison queries, Perplexity/ChatGPT appearance rate |
| BOFU | Local AI answer presence, Google AI Overviews citations for decision queries, conversion rate from AI-referred traffic |

Google Search Console provides AI Overviews data at the query level, allowing you to segment performance by query type and map it to funnel stage. Authoritas and SE Ranking track multi-engine citation rates that cover TOFU and MOFU stages.

## The cross-funnel content architecture

Full-funnel AI SEO requires deliberate content architecture -- not just individual pages, but a system:

**Hub pages** at the topic level cover the TOFU awareness layer. They are comprehensive, deeply structured, and schema-rich.

**Comparison and evaluation content** covers the MOFU layer. It answers the questions buyers ask when they are evaluating, and it links back to the hub for foundational knowledge.

**Local and specific pages** cover the BOFU layer. They carry location, credential, and service data in structured form.

**Internal linking connects all three layers.** A buyer who finds your TOFU content in a Perplexity citation should be able to navigate through to MOFU comparison content and BOFU contact or location pages without leaving your site.

## Prioritizing where to start

If you are building a full-funnel AI SEO program from scratch, the highest-leverage starting point varies by business type:

- **Local service businesses:** Start with BOFU. LocalBusiness schema, review management, and Google Business Profile optimization drive the highest-value citations first.
- **B2B services and SaaS:** Start with MOFU. Buyers research extensively at the consideration stage and this is where AI citations directly influence vendor shortlists.
- **E-commerce and product companies:** Start with TOFU. Category education content earns early brand exposure and establishes authority that carries into comparison queries.
