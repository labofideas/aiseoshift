The Google local pack — the three businesses with a map at the top of search results — drives more home services phone calls than any other surface on the internet. Paid LSA ads sit above it. AI Overviews now sit above the LSA ads. The local pack still converts at a higher rate than either.

## How the local pack actually ranks in 2026

Google has never fully documented the local pack algorithm, but the inputs we can measure and influence break down into five buckets:

### 1. Relevance

Does the business clearly match the query? Categories, services, business description, website content, and review text all factor in.

### 2. Proximity

How close is the business to the searcher? Less relevant for transactional searches where the searcher is at home and wants someone to come to them.

### 3. Prominence

Review count and recency, citation count, website authority, branded search volume.

### 4. Engagement

Photo views, profile clicks, direction requests, calls, post engagement. Active profiles outrank dormant ones.

### 5. Activity

Posts, photos, Q&A responses, review responses. Frequency and recency matter.

The home services businesses winning the local pack in 2026 are scoring well across all five buckets. The ones losing are scoring well only on relevance and proximity, and treating prominence, engagement, and activity as optional.

## Category strategy

Categories are the single most important GBP field for local pack eligibility. They directly determine which queries you are eligible to rank for.

### Primary category

Match your dominant trade:

- Plumber
- Electrician
- HVAC Contractor
- Roofing Contractor
- Drain Cleaning Service
- Water Damage Restoration Service

### Secondary categories

Every secondary category expands eligibility for additional query types. Common examples:

- **For plumbers:** Drain Cleaning Service, Water Heater Repair Service, Plumbing Supply Store (if applicable), Septic System Service
- **For electricians:** Lighting Contractor, Electrical Installation Service, Generator Shop (if applicable)
- **For HVAC:** Air Conditioning Contractor, Heating Contractor, Air Filter Supplier (if applicable), Furnace Repair Service

Do not add categories you do not actually serve. Google reconciles categories against the work shown in your reviews, photos, and website content. Mismatch gets the profile demoted or suspended.

## Service area precision

The service area field tells Google which queries your business is eligible to appear in based on the searcher's location.

Rules that work:

- **List actual cities and zip codes**, not "Metro Atlanta" or "the Greater Phoenix area." Specific lists outperform vague ones.
- **Cap at 20 cities per profile.** Service areas beyond 20 cities trigger Google's spam filtering.
- **For multi-location businesses, give each location its own GBP** with its own service area. Do not run one GBP claiming 80 cities of coverage.
- **Update annually.** Service area should reflect where you are actually dispatching trucks, not where you used to or hope to.

## Photo strategy

GBP photos affect local pack rankings directly. The composition matters.

### What to upload

- Logo (clean, square, transparent background)
- Cover photo (truck or office exterior with branding visible)
- Team photos (real photos, not stock)
- Work-in-progress and completed-job photos
- Branded truck and equipment photos
- Office interior and exterior

### Cadence

Add 5 to 15 new photos per month. Stale photo profiles (no new photos in 90+ days) lose local pack visibility.

### Tagging

Filename your photos descriptively before upload. "water-heater-installation-phoenix-az-2026.jpg" beats "IMG_4382.jpg" by a measurable margin in Google's image recognition pipeline.

## Posts as a ranking signal

GBP posts are the most underused surface in home services SEO. They appear inside the GBP panel, signal activity, and get crawled.

### Three post types that consistently move the needle

1. **Completed-job posts.** Photo, brief description of the work, neighborhood mentioned by name. Posted within 24 hours of job completion.
2. **Seasonal reminders.** "Time to schedule your AC tune-up before summer." "How to prevent frozen pipes." Two to four per month.
3. **Promotions.** Real, time-bound offers. Not "$50 off any service" forever — actual seasonal promotions with expiration dates.

### Cadence

One to two posts per week minimum. Profiles that post less than weekly lose local pack share to active competitors.

## Q&A as a pre-emptive surface

The GBP Q&A section is publicly editable. Most home services businesses ignore it. The smart ones seed their own questions and answers.

Common questions to pre-answer:

- What is your service area?
- Do you offer emergency or after-hours service?
- Are you licensed and insured?
- Do you offer financing?
- Are your estimates free?
- What is your warranty?

Seeded Q&A populates the GBP panel and feeds AI Overviews. It also reduces the chance of an unhelpful or hostile public question going unanswered.

## Citation cleanup

NAP citations (name, address, phone) across third-party directories still matter. Inconsistency hurts local pack rankings and confuses AI engines.

### The cleanup priority order

1. **Tier 1 directories:** Google, Bing, Apple Maps, Facebook, Yelp
2. **Industry-specific:** Angi, Home Advisor, BBB, Yellowpages
3. **Trade-specific:** Trade association directories, local chamber of commerce

Tools like BrightLocal, Whitespark, or Moz Local can audit and fix at scale. For a small operator, manual cleanup of the top 30 directories is sufficient.

## Schema markup for home services

Two schema types every home services site needs:

### LocalBusiness schema

```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "Plumber",
  "name": "Phoenix Plumbing Pros",
  "image": "https://example.com/logo.jpg",
  "telephone": "+1-602-555-0100",
  "address": {
    "@type": "PostalAddress",
    "streetAddress": "1500 E Camelback Rd",
    "addressLocality": "Phoenix",
    "addressRegion": "AZ",
    "postalCode": "85016",
    "addressCountry": "US"
  },
  "geo": {
    "@type": "GeoCoordinates",
    "latitude": 33.5099,
    "longitude": -112.0739
  },
  "areaServed": ["Phoenix", "Scottsdale", "Tempe", "Mesa"],
  "aggregateRating": {
    "@type": "AggregateRating",
    "ratingValue": "4.9",
    "reviewCount": "287"
  }
}
```

### Service schema

For each service you offer:

```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "Service",
  "serviceType": "Water Heater Installation",
  "provider": {
    "@type": "Plumber",
    "name": "Phoenix Plumbing Pros"
  },
  "areaServed": "Phoenix metro area",
  "description": "Tank and tankless water heater installation..."
}
```

## The compounding effect

The mistake most home services businesses make is treating local pack optimization as a one-time project. It is not. It is a daily activity that compounds over 12 to 24 months.

The businesses that are dominating their markets in 2026 started this work in 2023 or 2024. They did not do anything spectacular. They did the basics consistently. Reviews every week. Photos every week. Posts every week. Service-area pages added quarterly.

Two years in, the gap between them and competitors who treated this as optional is structural. It is very hard to close from behind.

## FAQs

### How long until the local pack changes show up?

Most signals reflect within 30 to 60 days. The local pack is not a single ranking algorithm — it samples and tests businesses based on engagement metrics, so the changes can be uneven for the first quarter before they stabilize.

### Can I rank in the local pack without a physical office?

Yes, with a service area business profile. You can choose to hide your address publicly while still verifying it privately with Google.

### What if I serve multiple cities but only have one office?

One GBP, service area listing every city you actually serve, individual service-area pages on your website for each. Many businesses do this successfully.

### Should I respond to fake or unfair reviews?

Always respond professionally. Refute false claims with facts. Do not get into arguments. Then report the review to Google through the standard reporting process. Most clearly fake reviews get removed within 2 to 4 weeks.
