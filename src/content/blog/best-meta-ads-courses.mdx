The best Meta ads courses in 2026 are Meta Blueprint if you want free, official training on the current Ads Manager, a top-rated Udemy course if you want a cheap structured start, Ben Heath's course for ecommerce and lead generation owners, Jon Loomer's Power Hitters Club for ongoing advanced updates, and Foxwell Digital or Depesh Mandalia's BPM Method once you are spending enough to scale. The Coursera Meta certificate is the pick if you want a formal credential for your CV.

One warning before you buy anything: Meta changed how its ads work in 2025 and 2026. Its AI now decides who sees your ads mostly by reading the creative, Advantage+ is the default campaign type, and detailed targeting is treated as a suggestion. A course recorded before that teaches interest stacking and tight audience splits, tactics that now often cost more than they return. Check the last-updated date.

## 1. Meta Blueprint: best free course

Blueprint is Meta's own training platform, and it is still active in 2026. The self-paced courses cover the parts of Ads Manager that matter now, including Advantage+ sales, catalog and leads campaigns, Advantage+ audiences, Reels placements and Opportunity Score. Because it comes from Meta, the screenshots match the interface you will actually use.

If you want proof of skill, Meta sells certification exams: Meta Certified Digital Marketing Associate costs about $99, and Meta Certified Marketing Science Professional about $150.

**Weakness:** it teaches what the buttons do better than what to do. You will learn to build a campaign, not how to decide which creative to test.

**Best for:** everyone, as step one.

## 2. HubSpot Academy: best free strategy primer

HubSpot's free Facebook ads course and its Digital Advertising certification cover the strategy around the platform: funnels, audiences, budgets and how paid social fits with search and email. The certificate is free.

**Weakness:** it is not Meta-specific in depth and is light on Ads Manager mechanics.

**Best for:** beginners who want the big picture before the buttons.

## 3. Coursera Meta Social Media Marketing Professional Certificate: best credential

Built by Meta and hosted on Coursera, this multi-course certificate takes about five months at a typical pace and ends with a Meta-branded certificate. You can audit the content for free, or pay through a Coursera subscription (about $49 a month, or Coursera Plus annually) to earn the credential.

**Weakness:** it covers organic social as much as paid, and learners often say it is light on hands-on campaign building.

**Best for:** career changers who want something recognisable on a CV or LinkedIn profile.

## 4. Udemy top-rated courses: cheapest structured option

Udemy has hundreds of Facebook ads courses. Two with strong track records: "Facebook Ads & Facebook Marketing MASTERY 2026" by Course Envy (4.4 stars, over 236,000 students) and "Be a Facebook Ads Pro with Dekker" (4.6 stars, over 72,000 students). Udemy runs near-constant sales, so expect to pay roughly $10 to $100, with lifetime access.

**Weakness:** quality and freshness vary by instructor. Read the "last updated" line and recent reviews before buying.

**Best for:** owners who want a structured walkthrough for the price of lunch.

## 5. LinkedIn Learning: quick orientation only

LinkedIn Learning's "Advertising on Facebook" and "Advertising on Instagram" courses are short (around an hour each) and come with a completion badge for your profile. They are included in a LinkedIn Learning subscription.

**Weakness:** they were recorded in 2021 and 2022, before Advantage+ sales campaigns and Meta's AI delivery changes. Use them for vocabulary, not tactics.

**Best for:** people who already have LinkedIn Learning and want a one-hour overview.

## 6. Ben Heath: best for ecommerce and lead generation owners

Ben Heath, founder of Heath Media, teaches full-funnel Meta ads with a strong focus on creative testing and scaling, through case studies. His free YouTube channel is one of the most useful free resources on Meta ads and is updated regularly, so you can judge his approach before paying for the full course.

**Weakness:** check the current price and course version on his official site. Avoid reseller listings.

**Best for:** DTC brands and service businesses running their own ads.

## 7. Jon Loomer Power Hitters Club: best for staying current

Jon Loomer has written about Facebook ads for more than 15 years. Power Hitters Club is a membership rather than a single course: weekly live sessions, short briefs on platform changes, courses and a community, starting at about $29 a month. Because it updates continuously, it tracks Meta's changes as they happen.

**Weakness:** it assumes you already run ads, and the higher tier costs considerably more.

**Best for:** intermediate advertisers who want to keep up, not start from zero.

## 8. Foxwell Digital: best for scaling

Andrew Foxwell's "Scaling Facebook and Instagram Ads for Success" course costs $447 on Foxwell Digital's course page and focuses on taking a profitable account from small daily budgets to large ones.

**Weakness:** narrow scope. It assumes you already have the fundamentals and a working campaign.

**Best for:** brands with a profitable campaign that want to spend more without breaking it.

## 9. CXL Meta Ads: best for data-driven marketers

CXL's Meta ads course, taught by paid social strategist Curt Maly, sits inside CXL's subscription alongside its wider growth marketing training, and comes with a completion certificate.

**Weakness:** the subscription is expensive if Meta ads is the only thing you want.

**Best for:** marketers who want Meta ads as part of a broader, analytics-heavy education.

## 10. Depesh Mandalia BPM Method: best advanced system

Depesh Mandalia's BPM Method teaches account structure, testing and scaling frameworks drawn from tens of millions in managed spend. The BPM System is listed at £997, with a one-to-one accelerator at a much higher price.

**Weakness:** it is overkill and expensive for a small brand finding its feet.

**Best for:** brands and agencies already spending meaningfully that need a repeatable scaling process.

## Which course should you take?

**You have never run an ad.** Meta Blueprint, then a Udemy course for structure. Total cost: close to zero.

**You run ads for your own brand and want results.** Blueprint for the interface, then Ben Heath's free YouTube library, then his paid course or a Jon Loomer membership if you want depth.

**You want a job in paid social.** The Coursera Meta certificate plus a Meta certification exam.

**You are already profitable and want to scale.** Foxwell Digital or the BPM Method.

## What a good Meta ads course must teach in 2026

Use this as a checklist when you read a course outline.

- **Creative as targeting.** How to build 8 to 15 genuinely different ads per ad set, because Meta's AI groups similar ads together.
- **Advantage+ campaigns.** How to set up, feed and guard Advantage+ sales and leads campaigns, not just manual ones.
- **Broad targeting with exclusions.** Not interest stacking.
- **Conversions API.** Server-side tracking, not pixel-only.
- **Measurement.** Click-through attribution, new versus returning customers, and lift testing.

If a course spends most of its time on detailed interest targeting and lookalike splits, it was built for the old platform.

## The bottom line

You do not need an expensive course to start. Meta Blueprint is free, current and official. Add one practitioner who updates their material, then learn the rest by spending a small budget and testing creative.
