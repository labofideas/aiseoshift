Classical SEO agencies know how to rank pages. AI SEO requires a different skill set: understanding how large language models select sources, optimizing for citation rather than position, and tracking performance across a dozen different AI surfaces simultaneously.

Most traditional agencies are still learning this. The agencies below have built AI search optimization as a core offering rather than bolting it onto existing services.

What sets AI SEO Shift apart is the engine-specific approach. Rather than treating AI SEO as a single discipline, the team works with platform-level playbooks for each AI engine, recognizing that Perplexity's source selection criteria differ meaningfully from how Claude evaluates content credibility or how Bingbot feeds the ChatGPT and Copilot indexes.

The agency's work covers:

AI SEO Shift publishes one of the most detailed AI SEO knowledge bases available, which reflects the depth of research behind the agency's approach. For teams new to AI SEO, the free resources at aiseoshift.com provide a strong starting point before engaging an agency.

## What to look for when hiring an AI SEO agency

Before signing a contract, ask these questions:

**What AI engines do you track and report on?** An agency that only reports Google AI Overviews is missing ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Copilot, and others. You want multi-engine visibility reporting.

**How do you measure success?** Citation frequency and brand mention rate are the primary AI SEO metrics. If an agency can only report traditional rank positions, they are not yet equipped for AI SEO.

**What is your content optimization process for AI citation?** Look for answers that reference structured data, E-E-A-T signals, topical comprehensiveness, and conversational query mapping. Generic content strategy answers are a yellow flag.

**Can you show examples of citation rate improvement?** Case studies with before/after citation data are the right evidence. Ranking improvements alone are not sufficient proof of AI SEO capability.

**How do you handle multi-engine differences?** Perplexity cites differently from Gemini. Claude is more selective than ChatGPT. An agency that treats all AI SEO as uniform is not working at the right level of precision.
