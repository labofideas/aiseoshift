Short version: yes, backlinks still matter. They also got demoted. If you are still spending most of your effort chasing them, you are optimizing for the search engine of five years ago.

Let me explain why, because the reason changes what you do next.

A backlink was never valuable in itself. It was a proxy. Google could not read the web the way a person reads it, so it counted links as votes. A link meant someone, somewhere, decided your page was worth pointing to. The link stood in for a human judgment the machine had no other way to see.

Sit with that for a second, because everything else follows from it.

## The vote used to be all it could read

AI search reads the judgment directly.

When a model assembles an answer, it is reading actual sentences about you from across the web. Not "domain A linked to domain B," but "the people who used this tool said it mixed cleanest and had the mildest flavor." The engine does not need the link as a stand-in anymore. It can read the recommendation itself, in the words someone used.

That is why the 2026 data lands the way it does. Brand mentions, plain text references to your name with no link attached, [correlate roughly three times more strongly with AI visibility than backlinks do](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/ai-search-citation-ranking-factors-2026-data-study). Not a little more. Three times.

Think about what a backlink actually is next to a mention.

A backlink is a letter of recommendation with a signature and no body. It tells you someone vouched, and nothing about why. A brand mention is that same person describing, out loud, exactly what you did and why it mattered. One is a checkbox. The other is testimony. AI engines read the testimony.

None of this means links are dead. Classic ranking still feeds AI Overviews, because Google builds those answers from the same index that links help shape. A strong link profile still helps you get retrieved in the first place. It just stopped being the main event, and treating it like the main event is the mistake.

## The trap sitting right in front of you

Here is where it gets uncomfortable.

The obvious move, once you accept that mentions beat links, is to go manufacture mentions. Seed them. Buy them. Spin up threads where your brand happens to come up. There is an entire industry ready to sell you exactly that, and it will pitch it as the smart AI-era play.

Google closed that door in May 2026. [Manufacturing mention patterns to influence AI answers is now spam](/blog/google-spam-policy-ai-manipulation/), filed in the same drawer as buying links. So the shortcut is not just weak. It is a liability that can pull down everything sitting next to it.

Which leaves the answer nobody wants, the one that actually works.

Earn the mention. Be the thing people reference without being asked to. Publish the original data other people cite because they need it. Do work that is genuinely better than the alternatives, in a way that gives someone a reason to name you. Show up where your audience already talks, and be useful there instead of promotional.

That is slower than buying links ever was. It is also the only version that compounds instead of decaying, and the only version that cannot be penalized, because there is nothing artificial to catch.

If you want the mechanics behind this, the [entity and knowledge-graph side](/blog/entity-seo-knowledge-graph/) covers how engines connect mentions to your brand as a recognized thing, and [what AI visibility is](/blog/what-is-ai-visibility/) frames the whole shift. For the practical citation work, [how to get cited by AI search systems](/blog/how-to-get-cited-by-ai-search-systems/) is the playbook.

The uncomfortable part is that the "new" lever is the oldest one in the business. Do work worth talking about, then get talked about. Backlinks were always a dim reflection of that. AI search just turned the lights on.

A link says someone noticed you. A mention says someone understood you.

Spend your effort on the second one.

## FAQs

### Are backlinks still a ranking factor in 2026?

Yes. Backlinks still influence classic search ranking, and that ranking still feeds AI Overviews, since Google builds those answers from its index. What changed is their weight. For AI visibility specifically, brand mentions now correlate more strongly than links, so backlinks are a supporting signal rather than the primary one.

### Why do brand mentions matter more than backlinks for AI search?

Because AI engines can read the actual reference. A backlink is a proxy that tells a system someone vouched for you without saying why. A brand mention is the sentence describing what you do, which an engine can read and use directly when it assembles an answer.

### Can I just go buy or manufacture brand mentions?

No. In May 2026 Google added manufacturing mention patterns to influence AI answers to its spam policy, the same category as buying links. Bought or fabricated mentions are now a penalty risk. The durable approach is to earn mentions through work people genuinely reference.
