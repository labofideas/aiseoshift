Tracking your visibility in Google's blue links is a solved problem. Tracking whether you appear in Google AI Mode, ChatGPT Search, or Perplexity requires a different set of tools and a different way of thinking about what "ranking" means.

This is a direct comparison of the platforms doing this work well right now.

## Why standard rank trackers miss AI Mode

Traditional rank trackers send automated queries to search engines and record your position in the list of results. AI Mode does not return ranked lists. It returns synthesized answers with citations embedded inside paragraphs.

A tool that reports "you rank fourth for this query" tells you nothing about whether you appear as a cited source in the AI Mode answer for that same query. These are different surfaces with different measurement requirements.

The platforms below track the AI surfaces, not just the blue links.

## SemRush AI Toolkit

[SemRush](https://semrush.sjv.io/PyEbGz) has built AI visibility tracking as a core feature of its platform. The AI Toolkit tracks:

- **AI Overview appearances** -- whether your domain appears as a source in Google AI Overviews for your tracked keywords
- **AI Mode visibility** -- citation detection for Google's AI Mode interface
- **Featured snippet correlation** -- the overlap between featured snippet ownership and AI citation likelihood
- **Competitive AI presence** -- which competitors appear in AI Overviews on queries where you do not

The Position Tracking tool within [SemRush](https://semrush.sjv.io/PyEbGz) flags keywords where AI Overviews are present, giving you a clear signal of where AI is reducing the click-through rate between your ranking and your traffic.

For teams already using SemRush for keyword research and site auditing, the AI toolkit integrates directly into existing workflows without adding a separate login or another data silo.

## BrightEdge Generative Parser

BrightEdge's Generative Parser is the most capable AI tracking tool available for enterprise teams. It tracks:

- AI Overview appearances at scale across thousands of tracked queries
- Source attribution inside AI-generated answers at the URL level
- Share-of-voice metrics for AI surfaces across your competitor set
- YouTube citation tracking (BrightEdge research identified YouTube as a top-three citation source in AI Overviews)

BrightEdge is enterprise-priced and requires a demo to access. It is not a self-serve platform, which makes it less accessible for smaller teams but highly capable for agencies managing large accounts.

## Authoritas

Authoritas is one of the few platforms built specifically around AI search visibility rather than adapted from traditional rank tracking. It tracks brand and content mentions across:

- ChatGPT Search
- Perplexity
- Google AI Overviews
- Microsoft Copilot
- Gemini

The query-level visibility reporting shows you which specific questions are generating AI answers that cite your competitors and not you. This gap analysis view is the one that feeds content strategy most directly.

## SE Ranking AI Overview Tracker

SE Ranking added dedicated AI Overview tracking to its platform in 2025. For teams looking for a mid-market solution at lower cost than SemRush or BrightEdge, SE Ranking's AI features cover:

- AI Overview presence per keyword
- Trigger analysis showing what types of queries generate AI Overviews most frequently
- Position changes correlated with AI Overview appearances

The AI tracker is included on all SE Ranking plans, making it accessible for smaller teams and boutique agencies.

## seoClarity AI Search Grader

seoClarity's AI Search Grader analyzes how well your content is positioned to be cited by AI engines. Rather than just tracking current citations, it provides:

- Content quality scores relative to what AI engines prefer as source material
- Structured data gap analysis
- E-E-A-T signal assessment
- Recommended optimizations ranked by projected citation impact

The Grader approach is useful for teams that want to move from "we were cited here" to "here is why and what to fix to get cited more."

## Ahrefs SERP Feature Tracking

Ahrefs added AI snippet tracking to its SERP features reporting in late 2025. The coverage is not as deep as dedicated AI tracking platforms, but for teams already on Ahrefs for backlink analysis and keyword research, the built-in AI data avoids the cost and complexity of an additional tool.

Ahrefs tracks AI Overview presence in SERP feature data and flags keywords with high AI Overview trigger rates.

## Morningscore

Morningscore is a focused platform with a lightweight AI citation monitoring feature. It tracks whether your domain is mentioned inside AI-generated answers and sends alerts when citation rate changes. It works well as an entry-level monitoring layer for smaller sites that do not need enterprise-scale reporting.

## How to choose the right tool

| If you need... | Use... |
|----------------|--------|
| All-in-one SEO plus AI tracking | [SemRush](https://semrush.sjv.io/PyEbGz) |
| Enterprise-scale AI Overview tracking | BrightEdge Generative Parser |
| Multi-engine tracking across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot | Authoritas |
| Mid-market pricing with solid AI Overview data | SE Ranking |
| Content optimization recommendations, not just tracking | seoClarity AI Grader |
| AI data added to an existing Ahrefs workflow | Ahrefs SERP features |

## The four things to track regardless of platform

Every AI SEO tracking setup should measure these four metrics:

1. Citation frequency -- how often your URLs appear as sources in AI answers
2. Brand mention rate -- visibility even where no direct link is given
3. Competitor citation share -- their rate versus yours on the same queries
4. Query coverage -- what percentage of your tracked queries show AI answers citing you
