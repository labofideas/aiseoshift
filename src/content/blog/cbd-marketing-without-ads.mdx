Every ecommerce brand hits traffic gaps. Most have a simple fix: spend more on ads. Pour budget into Google and Meta, buy the traffic back, move on.

CBD brands do not have that button.

Google Ads, Meta, and most major platforms restrict or ban CBD advertising. The fix everyone else reaches for is gone. That single fact should reshape how a CBD brand thinks about marketing, and most brands have not caught up to it.

![Marketing channels for a CBD brand, with paid ads effectively closed](/images/posts/cbd-marketing-without-ads-infographic.webp)

## The constraint is also the clarity

Not being able to buy traffic sounds like a disadvantage. In one way it is. In another, it removes the crutch that lets other brands avoid building anything durable.

A brand that can buy attention often never learns to earn it. A CBD brand has to earn it from day one. That forces investment in the assets that actually compound: content, authority, trust, and owned audience.

So the constraint pushes you toward the things that keep working after you stop paying. That is not a consolation prize. It is a better foundation.

## Where the growth has to come from

**Search is the engine.** Organic rankings and AI citations are the only channel that brings new customers at scale for a CBD brand. That makes [CBD AI SEO](/blog/cbd-ai-seo) not a line item but the center of the plan. Claim-safe content that ranks and gets cited is how strangers find you.

**Owned channels keep them.** Email and SMS are not blocked the way ads are. Once search brings someone in, owned channels are how you keep the relationship without paying a platform for re-access every time.

**Community builds trust at low cost.** A real audience that follows the brand, on email, on social, in person, is both a retention asset and a trust signal. In a category where trust is the whole game, that compounds.

**Earned media and partnerships extend reach.** Press, podcasts, practitioner relationships, and credible third-party coverage reach new people and, not incidentally, build the authority that AI engines look for before citing a brand.

None of these has the instant on-switch of an ad campaign. All of them keep paying after the work is done.

## The mindset shift

Stop waiting for the ad rules to change. They might, slowly, partially. Building your whole strategy around that hope is how years get wasted.

Build the engine that works under the constraint you actually have. A CBD brand that masters search, owns its audience, and earns trust will outgrow one that keeps treating the ad ban as a temporary inconvenience.

The brands that accept reality and build for it win. The ones waiting for permission lose the time.

## The takeaway

For CBD, search is not one channel among many. It is the channel. Build the content, authority, and trust that earn rankings and AI citations, keep customers through channels you own, and stop budgeting for ads you cannot run.
