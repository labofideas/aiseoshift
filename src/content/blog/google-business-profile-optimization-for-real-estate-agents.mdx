## Why GBP matters more than ever in the AI era

Three reasons GBP is more important in 2026 than it was in 2022:

1. **AI engines read GBP as authoritative.** When ChatGPT or Perplexity answers "best Realtor in Frisco TX," they pull from GBP categories, service area, review profile, and the structured data inside the listing.
2. **The local pack survived the AI Overview shift.** Even when AI Overviews appear above the SERP, the local three-pack still drives the highest volume of qualified phone calls in real estate.
3. **GBP is now the canonical source of truth.** Google reconciles your website schema, your social profiles, and your citations against the GBP. Inconsistency demotes you across all surfaces.

For the broader local SEO context, see [The Local SEO Checklist for 2026](/blog/local-seo-checklist-2026/).

## Section 1: Categories, get this right before anything else

Categories are the single most important GBP field. They determine which queries you are eligible to rank for.

**Primary category:** Always "Real Estate Agent" for individual agents, or "Real Estate Agency" for brokerages and teams.

**Secondary categories:** Add every category that matches your actual practice. Common ones for real estate agents:

- Real Estate Consultant
- Buyer's Agent
- Luxury Real Estate Agent
- Investment Service (if you sell investment properties)
- Property Management Company (only if you actually manage properties)

Do not add aspirational categories. If you have not closed a luxury deal, do not claim "Luxury Real Estate Agent." Google reconciles against your transaction history and review content, and false categorization gets your listing suspended.

## Section 2: Service area, be precise, not aspirational

The service area field tells Google and AI engines where you actually work. Two rules:

1. Only include cities and neighborhoods you have closed in.
2. Update annually as your practice shifts.

Agents who list 30 cities they have never transacted in look spammy to Google and confused to AI engines. Five to ten well-defined service areas with strong review and listing evidence beat 30 paper claims.

## Section 3: Business description

You get 750 characters. Use them. Most agents write three sentences and stop.

A strong description includes:

- Specific neighborhoods served
- Specialty (luxury, first-time buyers, investment, relocation)
- Years of experience and rough transaction count
- Designations (CRS, ABR, CLHMS)
- Unique value proposition (specific local market knowledge, fluency in a second language, niche expertise)

Avoid generic phrases like "providing excellent service to my clients." AI engines extract distinctive content. Boilerplate gets filtered.

## Section 4: Services

The services section is criminally underused by most agents. It is a list of specific services you offer, each with a description. AI engines use this list when matching specific buyer needs to agents.

Add services like:

- First-time home buyer consultation
- Luxury home buyer representation
- Investment property analysis
- Relocation assistance for [specific company campuses or industries]
- New construction buyer representation
- Listing services for [neighborhoods]

Each service should have a 50–80 word description. Reuse the same prose on your website service pages with [schema markup](/blog/real-estate-schema-markup-realestateagent-listing/) for double-counting.

## Section 5: Reviews, velocity and recency, not just volume

Review strategy is its own playbook. The high-leverage moves:

### Build a systematic post-close review request

Within 48 hours of close, send the client a short, personalized review request with a direct link to leave a Google review. Personalize the message, mention the neighborhood, the price point, or a specific moment from the transaction. Personalized requests convert at 40 percent. Generic ones convert at 12.

### Follow up once if no review after 14 days

A single follow-up roughly doubles your conversion rate. Two follow-ups annoys clients without significantly increasing the rate. Stop at one.

### Respond to every review within 48 hours

Both positive and negative. Responses signal to Google and AI engines that the business is active. Negative review responses signal that you take feedback seriously. Both move the needle on local pack rankings.

### Pay attention to recency

A profile with 30 reviews from the last six months outranks a profile with 90 reviews from 2022 to 2024. Google now weighs review recency heavily, and AI engines mirror that weighting.

### Never solicit reviews in bulk or with incentives

Bulk solicitation triggers Google's review filtering algorithms. Incentivized reviews (gift cards, discounts) violate guidelines and get the entire profile penalized. Always individual, always organic.

## Section 6: Posts, weekly, with intent

Google Business Profile posts are an underused surface. They appear inside the GBP panel, signal activity, and get crawled.

Three post types that work for real estate:

1. **New listing posts** (with photos, address, price, link)
2. **Market update posts** (monthly median price, days on market, inventory)
3. **Neighborhood spotlight posts** (link to your neighborhood landing pages)

Cadence: one to two posts per week. Stale profiles (no posts in 30+ days) get suppressed in the local pack.

## Section 7: Photos and video

GBP photos affect local pack rankings directly. The non-negotiables:

- Cover photo: a high-quality headshot or brokerage logo
- Logo: clean, square, transparent background
- Team photos: actual photos of you, not stock
- Interior and exterior office shots (if you have a physical office)
- Recent listing photos
- Neighborhood photos you personally shot

Aim for 30 to 80 total photos. Add new photos monthly. Tag photos with descriptive filenames before upload.

Video matters more than most agents realize. A 30 to 60 second "meet your agent" video on the GBP profile correlates with higher local pack visibility and stronger AI citation rates.

## Section 8: Q&A, pre-empt the questions

The GBP Q&A section is publicly editable. Most agents ignore it. The best agents pre-empt it by adding their own questions and answers.

Common questions to seed:

- What areas do you serve?
- Do you work with first-time home buyers?
- What is your typical commission?
- How long have you been in real estate?
- Do you speak any languages other than English?

Seeded Q&A populates the GBP panel and gets pulled into AI Overviews. It also reduces the chance of an unhelpful or hostile question from the public going unanswered.

## FAQs

### Should solo agents have their own GBP separate from the brokerage's?

Yes, if you can manage them both. Individual agent GBPs rank for personal-brand and specialty queries. The brokerage GBP ranks for the brand and team queries. They are not in competition.

### Can I have multiple GBPs if I serve multiple cities?

Only if you have a physical, staffed office in each city. Single-agent virtual presence in a city you do not have an office in violates Google's guidelines.

### What if I share an office with the brokerage?

Most major brokerages explicitly allow individual agent GBPs at the brokerage address. Verify with your broker before listing.

### How do I track GBP performance?

Use Google Business Profile Insights for impressions, calls, direction requests, and website clicks. Cross-reference with your CRM lead source field to map GBP traffic to closed transactions.
