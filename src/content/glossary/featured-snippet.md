A featured snippet is a direct answer Google pulls from a ranking page and displays at the top of the search results, above the first regular listing. It usually appears as a short paragraph, a bulleted or numbered list, or a table, depending on the question.

## What it is

When a query has a clear factual answer, Google selects a passage from a page that already ranks well and surfaces it in a highlighted box, often called position zero. The page is credited with a link, and the snippet is lifted close to verbatim rather than rewritten. Lists and tables tend to win for steps, rankings, or comparisons, while paragraphs win for definitions and short explanations.

## Why it matters

Featured snippets are the direct ancestor of AI Overviews. Both take an answer off a page and place it ahead of the links, and both can produce a zero-click result where the user reads the answer without visiting the source. The key difference is mechanism: a snippet quotes one page, while an Overview synthesizes several, and far more engines now do the synthesizing.

## How to win one

Answer the target question in a single tight passage placed near a matching heading, format steps and comparisons as lists or tables, and keep the answer factually precise so it reads well out of context.
