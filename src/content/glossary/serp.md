A SERP is the page of results a search engine shows in response to a query. It was once a simple list of ten blue links. Today it is a dense layout where organic results share space with many other elements.

## What appears on a modern SERP

- **Organic results**, the ranked links earned through SEO.
- **Paid results**, the ads marked as sponsored at the top and bottom.
- **AI Overviews**, Google's synthesized answer block that increasingly opens informational results.
- **Featured snippets**, a direct answer lifted from a ranking page.
- **Map pack**, the local three-business block for location queries.
- **People Also Ask, knowledge panels, image and video carousels**, and other rich features.

## Why the modern SERP matters

Each feature pushes traditional organic links further down and can answer the query before a click happens. A page can rank first in the organic results and still lose most of its traffic to an AI Overview or a featured snippet above it.

This is the core of the shift toward answer engines. Winning visibility now means earning placement inside these features, not just ranking in the link list. A page that is the cited source in an AI Overview gets brand exposure even when the click does not happen.

## How to compete

Identify which SERP features appear for your target queries, then structure content to win them: concise answers for snippets, schema for rich results, and strong local signals for the map pack. For the broader strategy, see [zero-click search strategy](/blog/zero-click-search-strategy/).
