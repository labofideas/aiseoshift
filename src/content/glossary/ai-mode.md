AI Mode is Google's conversational search experience, introduced at I/O 2025 and expanded through 2026. Instead of returning a ranked list of links, it holds a dialogue with the user and produces a synthesized answer, with citations to the pages it drew from.

## What it is

AI Mode behaves less like a search box and more like an assistant. A single question can trigger several searches behind the scenes, a technique Google describes as query fan-out, where the system breaks the request into parts, retrieves sources for each, and weaves the results into one response. Follow-up questions keep the context, so the conversation refines rather than restarts.

## Why it matters

For many query types AI Mode is becoming the default surface, displacing the traditional list of blue links. That shifts the goal from ranking first to being one of the sources the synthesized answer cites. Because the engine compresses many results into a short answer, fewer pages get surfaced, raising the bar for inclusion.

## How to adapt

Write content that answers specific sub-questions cleanly, since fan-out retrieval rewards pages that match narrow intents. Strong entity signals and structured data help Google connect your content to the right part of a multi-step query. Keep facts current and clearly dated, because conversational answers favor sources that look fresh and verifiable.
