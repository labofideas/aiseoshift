AI Overviews are the AI-generated answer boxes Google places above the classical organic results for a growing share of queries. They summarize an answer in a few sentences and attach links to the sources the summary was built from.

## What it is

An AI Overview reads a query, retrieves a set of relevant pages, and writes a short synthesized summary with attributed source links. The pages it draws from are predominantly ones already ranking in the top 10 to 20 organic results, so classical ranking remains the entry ticket. The summary itself is generated, not copied, which means a page can inform an Overview without any single sentence being quoted verbatim.

## Why it matters

Overviews push answers higher on the page and absorb attention that once went to the first organic listing. For informational queries this often produces a zero-click outcome, where the user reads the summary and never clicks through. The trade is visibility through citation rather than traffic through a click, so being one of the linked sources is the new prize.

## How to adapt

Earn top organic rankings first, because that is the pool Overviews draw from. Then make individual passages quotable: clear, factual, and able to stand alone. For deeper coverage, see [AI search optimization](/seo/ai-search-optimization/).
