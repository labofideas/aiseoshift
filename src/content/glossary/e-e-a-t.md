E-E-A-T stands for Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness. It is the framework Google's quality raters use to judge content, expanded from the older E-A-T in 2022 to add Experience as a distinct signal.

## What it is

The framework asks whether content comes from someone who has actually done the thing (Experience), who knows the subject deeply (Expertise), who is recognized as a go-to source (Authoritativeness), and whether the page and its publisher can be relied on (Trustworthiness). Trust sits at the center, since accurate, honest content is the point the other three support.

## Why it matters for AI search

AI answer engines lean on E-E-A-T signals when deciding which sources to cite, and they lean on them hardest for high-stakes topics. In medical, legal, and financial verticals, often called Your Money or Your Life subjects, an engine is far more likely to cite a page with clear authorship, credentials, and a trustworthy publisher behind it. Weak trust signals are a common reason strong content goes uncited.

## How to apply it

Name your authors and show their qualifications, cite primary sources, keep facts current, and make the publishing organization easy to verify. For deeper coverage, see [our E-E-A-T guide](/seo/eeat/).
