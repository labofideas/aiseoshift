AISEOSHIFT / OWNER'S CHECKLIST
During the rollout

Watch your business.
Not just your rankings.

Six checks to keep the search data in context.

01

Impressions & clicks

Review important landing pages.
Are people seeing and visiting them?

02

Queries, position & CTR

Check which searches changed.
Averages can hide the pattern.

03

Brand vs. discovery

Separate brand-name searches
from new-customer searches.

04

Country & device

Find out whether the change
affects one audience or many.

05

Leads, orders & revenue

Connect organic search visits
to outcomes that pay the bills.

06

Technical health

Check availability, indexing,
forms and checkout.

A traffic drop alone does not establish the cause.
Record changes now; assess update impact after the rollout settles.
